# Chapter 40: Plunder & Cargo Tables

When the smoke clears and the enemy ship begins to sink, the victor takes the spoils. Use these d100 tables to quickly generate randomized cargo, loot, and contraband whenever players successfully board and salvage an enemy vessel.

To determine what a ship is carrying, roll 1d4 times on Table A, 1d2 times on Table B, and 1 time on Table C.

---

## Table A: Generic Cargo Hold
This table represents the standard trade goods, supplies, and mundane cargo found in the main holds of most merchant and faction vessels.

| d100 | Cargo Discovered | Value |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 01-10 | **Spoiled Rations:** Barrels of hardtack and salted pork that have been tainted by seawater. | 0 gp |
| 11-20 | **Iron-Wood Timber:** 10 bundles of hardened timber used for repairing ships. | 50 gp |
| 21-30 | **Cheap Rum:** 5 large barrels of harsh, burning pirate liquor. | 25 gp |
| 31-40 | **Whale Oil:** 20 sealed jars of highly flammable blubber oil. | 100 gp |
| 41-50 | **Basic Munitions:** 50 iron cannonballs and 3 kegs of standard black powder. | 150 gp |
| 51-60 | **Spices:** A small, locked crate containing rare saffron and cinnamon. | 300 gp |
| 61-70 | **Fine Silks:** 5 bolts of high-quality, water-resistant spider-silk. | 400 gp |
| 71-80 | **Medical Supplies:** Healer's kits, bandages, and a dozen vials of basic antitoxin. | 100 gp |
| 81-90 | **Raw Ore:** Crates filled with unrefined iron and copper ore. | 200 gp |
| 91-99 | **Precious Metals:** A locked strongbox containing silver and gold trade bars. | 800 gp |
| 100 | **The Motherlode:** A perfectly preserved, massive pearl harvested from a Leviathan. | 2,500 gp |

## Table B: The Smuggler's Hold
Found hidden beneath false floors or in lead-lined compartments. This represents the highly illegal, dangerous, or heavily taxed goods that Free Captains and Consortium smugglers transport.

| d100 | Contraband Discovered |
| :---: | :--- |
| 01-15 | **Volatile Aether Dust:** 3 pouches of glowing blue powder. Can be snorted for a temporary +2 to spell save DCs, but the user takes 2d6 necrotic damage. Highly illegal in Dominion waters. |
| 16-30 | **Stolen Masterworks:** 1d4 crates of plundered, high-society artwork. Fencing these requires passing a DC 16 Charisma (Deception) check, or drawing the ire of the Consortium. (Value: 500 gp each). |
| 31-45 | **Unstable Aether Core:** A crackling, football-sized energy core. Can be used to power a massive ship weapon once, or sold on the black market for 1,000 gp. Highly explosive. |
| 46-60 | **Drow Slumber-Poison:** 5 vials of a thick purple liquid. A creature hit by a weapon coated in this poison must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution save or fall asleep for 1 hour. |
| 61-75 | **Forged Letters of Marque:** Blank, officially sealed documents that grant the bearer legal permission to act as privateers for the Crimson Dominion. Highly valuable to pirates. |
| 76-90 | **Precursor Tech Fragments:** Rusted, incomprehensible clockwork pieces from a lost civilization. Tinkers and artificers will pay handsomely (800 gp) for them. |
| 91-99 | **A Captured Monster:** A locked iron cage containing a highly aggressive, starved monstrosity (e.g., a young Arachna-Drider or Kelp-Hydra). |
| 100 | **A High-Value Hostage:** A bound and gagged noble or high-ranking faction officer. Ransoming them could yield 5,000 gp, but will undoubtedly start a war. |

## Table C: The Captain's Quarters
The personal stash, lockboxes, and secrets kept by the commander of the defeated vessel.

| d100 | Personal Loot |
| :---: | :--- |
| 01-15 | **The Ship's Ledger:** A detailed book containing the routes, codes, and passwords for several faction strongholds. (Grants advantage on navigation and infiltration checks). |
| 16-30 | **A Potion Stash:** 1d4 *Potions of Greater Healing* and 1 *Potion of Water Breathing*. |
| 31-45 | **Captain's Fine Clothes:** An incredibly gaudy, heavily enchanted coat. (*+1 Studded Leather Armor*). |
| 46-60 | **A Treasure Map:** A cryptic, blood-stained map pointing to an uncharted island in the Shattered Expanse where a legendary Pirate Lord buried their loot. |
| 61-75 | **The Magic Compass:** A *Compass of the Drowned* (See Chapter 38). |
| 76-90 | **Aether-Charged Weapon:** The captain's personal sidearm (A *+1 Aether-Charged Cutlass* or *Powder-Mage's Flintlock*). |
| 91-99 | **A Ring of Keys:** A keyring that opens every lock on the ship, plus a strange, glowing bone-key that radiates necromantic magic (Purpose unknown). |
| 100 | **A Cursed Relic:** The captain was hiding a terrifying artifact of immense power. Roll 1d4. (1: *The Black Spot Doubloon*, 2: *Crown of the Deep Mother*, 3: *Aether-Core Heart*, 4: *Drowned Captain's Log*). |
