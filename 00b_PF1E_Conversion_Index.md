# Appendix: Pathfinder 1E Conversion Index

Welcome to the Pathfinder 1E conversion index for the Wayfinder Sourcebook! While the core text of this book is written using strict D&D 3.5e terminology, it was designed with seamless Pathfinder 1E cross-compatibility in mind. 

You can easily run this setting in PF1E by following these broad conversions and referencing the `> **[PF1E Conversion Sidebar]**` blocks located underneath specific mechanics throughout the text.

## Part 1: Rules & Classes (Chapters 1-10)
* **Skills:** Spot, Listen, and Search are condensed into **Perception**. Hide and Move Silently are condensed into **Stealth**. Balance, Tumble, and Jump are condensed into **Acrobatics**.
* **Base Attack Bonus:** The Wayfinder class uses PF1E's standard fractional BAB scaling.

## Part 2: Lore (Chapters 11-20)
* Lore and worldbuilding remain mechanically agnostic. No conversions are required.

## Part 3: Naval Combat (Chapters 21-30)
* **Ship Maneuvers:** Any opposed Grapple, Bull Rush, or Ram checks during boarding or ship-to-ship actions can be resolved using PF1E's unified **CMB (Combat Maneuver Bonus)** and **CMD (Combat Maneuver Defense)** mechanics.
* **Checks:** Replace all mentions of Search checks to find smuggler compartments with **Perception**, and Hide checks with **Stealth**.

## Part 4: Bestiary & Items (Chapters 31-40)
* **Statblocks:** Every monster statblock contains a `> **[PF1E Conversion Sidebar]**` listing its calculated CMB, CMD, and condensed skills (e.g., Perception and Stealth) to replace the 3.5e Grapple modifier and split skills.
* **Feats:** PF1E feat progressions and effects (such as the scaling of Power Attack) should be used in place of the 3.5e versions.

## Part 5: Adventures (Chapters 41-50)
* **DCs:** Treat all skill checks for Spot/Search as **Perception**, and Hide/Move Silently as **Stealth**.
* **Hazards:** The Fortitude, Reflex, and Will saves for hazards (like Aether Radiation or Ash-Sickness) operate identically across both systems.
