# Chapter 37: Naval Loot & Ship Capture Tables

When a Wayfinder Corsair crew successfully captures an enemy vessel, the Game Master can use the following tables to generate the contents of the cargo hold and the captain's private coffers. 

To use these tables, first determine the **Class of the Captured Ship**, which dictates how many rolls the GM makes on each table.

## 1. Ship Class Loot Multipliers

| Ship Class | Examples | Bulk Cargo Rolls | Munitions Rolls | Contraband Rolls | Captain's Coffer Rolls |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Light** | Skiff, Sloop, Interceptor | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 |
| **Medium** | Cutter, Corsair, Brig | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| **Heavy Cargo** | Merchant Galleon, Cog | 5 | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| **Warship** | Heavy Cruiser, Frigate | 2 | 4 | 1 | 2 |
| **Flagship** | Ironclad, Dreadnought | 3 | 5 | 3 | 3 |
