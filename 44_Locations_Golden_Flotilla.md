# Chapter 44: The Golden Flotilla (Consortium Hub)

*Not a static island, but a massive fleet of opulent mega-ships chained together into a moving city of commerce.*

The Grand Merchant Consortium does not trust land. Land can be besieged, blockaded, or destroyed by earthquakes. Instead, their capital is the Golden Flotilla, a collection of dozens of massive dreadnoughts and galleons chained together by bridges of solid gold and iron. It is the richest moving target in the world, heavily guarded by mercenary fleets.

### Physical Description
The Flotilla is blindingly bright during the day, reflecting sunlight off gilded hulls and polished brass cannons. At night, it glows with magical lanterns, looking like a constellation fallen to the sea. The central ships are massive floating palaces, while the outer ring consists of heavily armed escort frigates.

### Key NPCs
- **The Vance Brothers:** A pair of ruthlessly efficient Merchant Lords who oversee the Great Exchange. They finish each other's sentences and share a magical bond. (See Chapter 37 for statblock).
- **Captain Alistair Vance:** The commander of the mercenary defense fleet. He cares only for coin and will turn a blind eye to piracy if the bribe is high enough.

### Local Laws & Dangers
- **The Iron Ledger:** Every transaction, conversation, and breath on the Flotilla is taxed. Failure to pay docking fees or tariffs results in immediate seizure of your vessel.
- **Mercenary Law:** The city is policed by Gold Cloaks. They are well-paid and completely incorruptible by small sums, but will absolutely look the other way for a king's ransom. Any act of violence disrupts trade, which is a capital offense.

### Points of Interest

#### 1. The Great Exchange
The largest ship in the fleet, functioning as a massive auction house. Here, entire islands, cursed artifacts, and mercenary armies are bought and sold. The bidding wars are cutthroat, sometimes literally. *Adventure Hook: The players must enter a high-stakes auction to win back a stolen piece of their ship's core.*

#### 2. The Vault-Ships
Specially designed, heavily armored barges that contain the Consortium's liquid assets. They sit low in the water, practically submarines, and are guarded by Abjuration wards and Ledger-Mages.

#### 3. The Velvet Lounge
A high-end casino on one of the pleasure yachts. High-stakes card games, magical roulette, and political backstabbing are the main attractions. It is neutral ground where rival faction leaders sometimes meet in secret to negotiate.
