# Chapter 26: Custom Player Options

The Shattered Expanse is a world defined by the sea, ancient magic, and cutthroat factions. Standard backgrounds and class archetypes can be adapted, but to truly root your character in the lore of the Charted Waters, consider the following setting-specific options.

---

## 1. Setting-Specific Backgrounds

### Dominion Legionnaire
You were a soldier, inquisitor, or logistics officer for the Crimson Dominion. You are used to strict hierarchy, ironclad law, and the belief that order must be imposed on a chaotic world. You may still be active duty, or you may be a deserter fleeing the Iron Arbiter's wrath.

- **Skill Proficiencies:** Intimidation, Athletics
- **Tool Proficiencies:** One type of gaming set, land vehicles
- **Equipment:** A legionnaire's uniform, an insignia of rank, a set of bone dice, and a pouch containing 15 gp.
- **Feature: Military Bureaucracy.** You know how to navigate the complex checkpoints and red tape of the Crimson Dominion. You can often secure an audience with lower-to-mid-tier Dominion officers, and you can comfortably requisition basic, non-magical supplies (rations, standard ammunition, simple weapons) from a Dominion garrison, provided you are in good standing.

### Consortium Smuggler
You grew up in the shadows of the Grand Merchant Consortium. While the Guildmasters deal in golden ledgers, you deal in the black market. You know how to move illicit goods, forge manifests, and avoid harbor inspections.

- **Skill Proficiencies:** Deception, Sleight of Hand
- **Tool Proficiencies:** Forgery kit, Navigator's tools
- **Equipment:** A crowbar, dark common clothes with a hidden pocket, a forged shipping manifest, and a pouch containing 15 gp.
- **Feature: Black Market Contacts.** Whenever you arrive in a major port or settlement, you know the subtle signs to look for to locate a fence or a smuggler's den. You can easily find buyers for stolen or illegal goods, and you can purchase illicit items without drawing the attention of local authorities.

### Silk House Exile
You are a survivor of the terrifying Drowned Silk Houses. You might be a rogue Drow who rejected the Deep Mother, or a surface-dweller who was captured and managed to escape the abyssal slave pens. You carry the trauma of the deep dark with you.

- **Skill Proficiencies:** Stealth, Survival
- **Language:** Undercommon
- **Equipment:** A dagger made of black coral, a set of dark, tattered clothes, a trinket from the deep ocean, and a pouch containing 10 gp.
- **Feature: Sunless Survivor.** You have a knack for finding safe harbor in subterranean, underwater, or extremely hostile environments. You can easily identify safe places to rest in natural cave systems or ruins, and you have an instinctive sense of depth and direction when deprived of sunlight.

### Spirit Keeper Warden
You are a defender of the Verdant Expanse. You revere the Slumbering Leviathans and view the industrialization of the empires as a blight upon the world. You have spent your life listening to the song of the ocean.

- **Skill Proficiencies:** Nature, Animal Handling
- **Tool Proficiencies:** Herbalism kit
- **Equipment:** A staff carved from driftwood, a vial of pure aether-water, a set of traveler's clothes, and a pouch containing 10 gp.
- **Feature: Voice of the Reef.** You can interpret the subtle signs of sea-life (the migration of fish, the blooming of kelp, the songs of whales) to accurately predict mundane weather patterns for the next 24 hours, and you can sense when a Slumbering Leviathan is beginning to stir in the region.

### Independent Salvager
You belong to the loose collective of scavengers who dive into the treacherous Unwritten Sea to retrieve artifacts of the First Captains. You are part historian, part mechanic, and part daredevil.

- **Skill Proficiencies:** Investigation, History
- **Tool Proficiencies:** Tinker's tools, Thieves' tools
- **Equipment:** A set of tinker's tools, a strange, broken technological artifact, a set of sturdy diving clothes, and a pouch containing 15 gp.
- **Feature: Junk Tinkerer.** You can scavenge parts from your surroundings to temporarily repair mundane broken items (like a snapped axle, a rusted lock, or a leaky hull) or jury-rig simple, single-use tools (like a makeshift pulley or a temporary patch). These repairs last for 24 hours before breaking again.

## 2. New Subclasses

### Bard: College of the Shanty
Bards of the College of the Shanty are not delicate lute-strummers playing in royal courts. They are loud, rhythmic leaders who use booming voices and percussive instruments to keep a crew working in perfect synchronization. Their magic is woven into the beat of the drum and the stomp of the boot, capable of turning a disorganized mob into a unified, terrifying force.

**Bonus Proficiencies**
When you join the College of the Shanty at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with water vehicles and one type of artisan's tools (such as carpenter's tools or cook's utensils). If you are already proficient, you can choose another tool proficiency.

**Heave Away**
At 3rd level, you can use your Bardic Inspiration to coordinate your allies. When an ally rolls a Bardic Inspiration die you gave them, they gain temporary hit points equal to the number rolled + your Charisma modifier. Furthermore, if they use the die to make a melee weapon attack, they can push the target 5 feet away from them on a hit.

**Rhythm of the Sea**
At 6th level, your voice cuts through the chaos of battle. As an action, you can begin a rhythmic chant. For 1 minute, as long as you maintain concentration (as if concentrating on a spell), you and friendly creatures within 30 feet of you who can hear you ignore difficult terrain and have advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.

**Thunderous Chorus**
At 14th level, you can unleash the accumulated energy of your crew's synchronization. When a creature within 30 feet of you deals damage to a target while under the effect of your *Rhythm of the Sea*, you can use your reaction to expend one use of your Bardic Inspiration. Roll the die; the target takes additional thunder damage equal to two rolls of your Bardic Inspiration die, and must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your spell save DC or be knocked prone.

### Barbarian: Path of the Leviathan
Barbarians who walk the Path of the Leviathan emulate the terrifying, crushing power of the ancient sea beasts that slumber beneath the waves. They do not just channel anger; they channel the abyssal pressure, the freezing cold, and the unstoppable mass of the deep ocean.

**Abyssal Adaptation**
Starting when you choose this path at 3rd level, you gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed, and you can breathe underwater. Additionally, you ignore any drawbacks caused by a deep underwater environment.

**Crushing Pressure**
At 3rd level, your rage manifests as a field of heavy, aquatic pressure. While you are raging, the ground within 10 feet of you is difficult terrain for your enemies. If an enemy starts its turn within this area, its speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of its next turn.

**Leviathan's Mass**
At 6th level, you can channel the unstoppable momentum of a sea beast. While raging, you have advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks made to grapple or shove a creature. Additionally, you count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.

**Depths of Despair**
At 10th level, you can exude the terrifying, cold isolation of the sunless sea. As an action, you can force all creatures of your choice within 30 feet of you to make a Wisdom saving throw (DC equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier). On a failed save, a creature is frightened of you for 1 minute. A frightened creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. Once you use this feature, you cannot do so again until you finish a long rest.

**Tsunami Strike**
At 14th level, when you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack while raging, you can unleash a shockwave of force. The target and all enemies within 15 feet of it behind it must make a Strength saving throw (DC equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier). On a failed save, they take 4d6 bludgeoning damage and are knocked prone. On a successful save, they take half damage and are not knocked prone. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

## 3. Setting-Specific Feats

If your DM allows the use of feats, you may choose the following options, which represent unique adaptations to the Shattered Expanse.

### Sea Legs
You have spent so much time on the unsteady decks of ships that the rolling waves feel more natural than solid ground.
- Increase your Dexterity or Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You have advantage on saving throws and ability checks against being knocked prone.
- Climbing rigging or swimming does not cost you extra movement.

### Aether-Scarred
You survived prolonged exposure to the chaotic, magical radiation of the Unwritten Sea, leaving you physically marked and magically altered.
- Increase your Constitution or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You gain resistance to either Force or Lightning damage (your choice when you take this feat).
- You learn the *thaumaturgy* cantrip. When you cast it, the effects often have a slightly mutated, unsettling, or oceanic flair (e.g., your voice sounds like grinding tectonic plates, or the flames you flicker turn bioluminescent blue). You can cast this cantrip using Constitution as your spellcasting ability.
