# Chapter 27: Naval Combat & Ship Mechanics

The Shattered Expanse is a world of oceans, storms, and piracy. While 5th Edition combat excels at close-quarters skirmishes, simulating ship-to-ship combat requires a system that makes the vessel feel like a character in its own right, while keeping the players—the officers—at the center of the action.

The system presented here is **cinematic and role-driven**. Rather than treating ships as sluggish grid-tokens, this system focuses on the dramatic maneuvers of the vessel and the heroic actions of the crew on deck.

---

## 1. The Anatomy of a Ship

Every ship in the Expanse has a set of statistics similar to a creature, but simplified to keep combat moving quickly.

- **Armor Class (AC):** Represents the thickness of the hull and the ship's maneuverability.
- **Hull Points (HP):** The ship's hit points. When a ship reaches 0 HP, it begins to sink (it does not sink instantly, giving the crew time to evacuate or magically repair it).
- **Speed:** The ship's movement rate, usually measured in knots (for travel) or feet per round (for combat).
- **Damage Threshold:** If a single attack deals damage less than this threshold, the ship takes no damage (the attack glances off the armor).
- **Artillery:** The cannons, ballistae, or aether-weapons mounted on the ship.

### Example Ship Profiles

#### Free Captain Sloop
*A fast, highly maneuverable vessel favored by smugglers and pirates.*
- **AC:** 15
- **Hull Points:** 250
- **Damage Threshold:** 10
- **Speed:** 60 ft.
- **Artillery:** 4 Broadside Cannons (2 per side). *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, range 120/480 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3d10 bludgeoning damage.

#### Dominion Ironclad
*A heavily armored, steam-and-aether powered beast of war.*
- **AC:** 18
- **Hull Points:** 500
- **Damage Threshold:** 20
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **Artillery:** 1 Heavy Aether-Cannon (Front). *Ranged Spell Attack:* +8 to hit, range 300/1200 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6d10 force damage. 4 Broadside Cannons (2 per side).

#### Consortium Merchant Galleon
*A massive, slow, but heavily armed cargo vessel.*
- **AC:** 14
- **Hull Points:** 400
- **Damage Threshold:** 15
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Artillery:** 8 Broadside Cannons (4 per side).

#### Drow Submersible (The "Abyssal Skiff")
*A terrifying vessel made of black coral that travels beneath the waves.*
- **AC:** 16
- **Hull Points:** 200
- **Damage Threshold:** 10
- **Speed:** 40 ft. (surface or underwater)
- **Artillery:** 2 Torpedo Tubes. *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target (underwater only). *Hit:* 4d12 piercing damage.

## 2. Officer Roles & Combat Flow

Naval combat happens in standard initiative. The ship itself does not roll initiative; instead, it moves and acts on the turn of the **Helmsman**. 

During combat, players take on specific **Officer Roles**. An officer can use their action to take a standard 5e action (like casting a spell or shooting a bow), OR they can take a special **Ship Action** associated with their role.

### The Captain
The leader of the vessel, responsible for morale and coordination.
- **Ship Action - "Fire at Will!":** You grant advantage to all artillery attacks made by your ship until the start of your next turn.
- **Ship Action - "Brace for Impact!":** You shout a warning. Until the start of your next turn, the ship gains resistance to all bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from ship weapons or environmental hazards, and the crew has advantage on saving throws to avoid being knocked prone.

### The Helmsman
The one steering the ship. The ship moves on the Helmsman's turn.
- **Free Action - Move:** You use the ship's speed to move it across the battlefield.
- **Ship Action - "Hard to Port/Starboard!":** You make a DC 15 Dexterity (Water Vehicles) check. On a success, the ship can immediately turn up to 90 degrees and move up to half its speed, avoiding an incoming hazard or lining up a perfect broadside.
- **Ship Action - Ramming Speed:** You move the ship in a straight line toward a target. If you hit, make a melee weapon attack (+6). On a hit, both ships take 6d10 bludgeoning damage, but if your ship has a reinforced prow, it takes half damage.

### The Gunner
The master of the ship's artillery.
- **Ship Action - Fire Artillery:** You direct the crew to fire one battery of weapons (e.g., all cannons on the port side). Make an attack roll for the battery. You can add your proficiency bonus and your Intelligence or Dexterity modifier to the attack roll instead of the ship's base modifier.
- **Ship Action - Targeted Shot:** You aim for a specific part of the enemy ship (e.g., the mast, the rudder). Make an artillery attack with disadvantage. If it hits, you cripple that component. (A crippled mast halves speed; a crippled rudder prevents turning).

### The Bosun
The chief engineer and disciplinarian, keeping the ship and crew functioning.
- **Ship Action - Emergency Repairs:** You direct the damage control crew. Make a DC 15 Strength or Intelligence (Carpenter's Tools) check. On a success, the ship regains 4d10 Hull Points. You can only successfully use this action three times per day.
- **Ship Action - Overdrive:** You push the engines or the riggers beyond their limits. The ship's speed increases by 20 feet until the end of the round, but the ship takes 1d10 damage from the strain.

### The Surgeon
The ship's medic, vital during boarding actions and artillery barrages.
- **Ship Action - Triage:** You rush across the deck, stabilizing the dying and treating the wounded. You can cast a healing spell as a Ship Action with doubled range, or you can expend a use of a healer's kit to heal a creature for 1d6 + 4 hit points plus a number equal to the creature's maximum number of Hit Dice.

## 3. Ship Upgrades & Customization

Players who acquire a ship (whether by stealing a Dominion vessel, buying one from the Consortium, or salvaging a wreck) can spend gold and downtime to upgrade it.

| Upgrade | Cost | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aether-Core Engine** | 5,000 gp | The ship no longer relies entirely on wind. Base speed increases by +10 ft., and the ship can move regardless of weather. |
| **Smuggler's Compartment** | 1,000 gp | Adds a hidden cargo hold that requires a DC 20 Investigation check to find. |
| **Reinforced Iron Prow** | 2,500 gp | The ship gains resistance to damage taken from ramming, and deals an extra 2d10 damage when it rams a target. |
| **Mithral Plating** | 10,000 gp | Increases the ship's AC by +2 and Damage Threshold by +5, without slowing it down. |
| **Diving Bell** | 1,500 gp | A pressurized sphere that can be lowered via crane to the ocean floor, holding up to 4 Medium creatures. |

## 4. Environmental Hazards

The ocean is often more dangerous than the enemy fleet. The DM can roll on this table at the start of combat, or trigger hazards dynamically.

| d6 | Hazard | Effect |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Rogue Wave:** | A 40-foot tall wave sweeps the battlefield. The Helmsman must make a DC 16 Water Vehicles check. On a failure, the ship is pushed 60 feet and takes 4d10 damage. All creatures on deck must make a DC 14 Str save or be swept overboard. |
| 2 | **Aether-Storm:** | Lightning strikes randomly. At initiative count 20, 1d4 random targets (ships or creatures) take 3d10 lightning damage. |
| 3 | **Jagged Reefs:** | The water is treacherously shallow. Moving at more than half speed requires a DC 15 Water Vehicles check; failure results in a hull breach (2d10 damage and speed reduced to 0 until repaired). |
| 4 | **Leviathan's Wake:** | A massive beast stirs below. The water churns into difficult terrain for all ships. Submersibles cannot function in the turbulence. |
| 5 | **Ghost Fog:** | Visibility drops to 30 feet. Artillery attacks beyond this range automatically miss. |
| 6 | **Dead Calm:** | The wind dies completely. Ships without an Aether-Core engine have their speed reduced to 10 feet (rowing speed). |
