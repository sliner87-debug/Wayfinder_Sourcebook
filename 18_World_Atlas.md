# Chapter 18: The World Atlas

*“The world did not end. It merely shattered. What was once a continent of empires is now an ocean of possibilities.”* — First Captain Alaric Thorne

The **Shattered Expanse** is the defining geographical reality of the setting. Roughly 800 years ago, a cataclysm known as the **Sundering Tide** obliterated the ancient supercontinent, fragmenting it into tens of thousands of islands scattered across an endless, turbulent ocean.

## Charted Waters vs. The Unwritten Sea

The ocean is roughly divided into two distinct logistical and magical zones.

### The Charted Waters (35% of the Expanse)
The Charted Waters represent the "civilized" or at least "survivable" regions of the world. These are the major trade routes, the secured archipelagoes, and the naval territories claimed by the dominant factions. 
- **Navigational Reality:** Standard compasses work here. The weather behaves according to predictable patterns, and the ley lines are stable enough to allow for reliable arcane travel.
- **The Great Magical Trade Network:** This is where the bulk of the Pirate Renaissance takes place. Armadas clash over taxation routes, and corsairs raid the fat merchant galleons of the empires.

### The Unwritten Sea (65% of the Expanse)
Beyond the edges of the faction maps lies The Unwritten Sea. This is the untamed, shifting, and wildly magical frontier. 
- **Navigational Reality:** Non-magical compasses spin wildly. Islands move with the tides, and the stars occasionally change position. Navigating the Unwritten Sea requires a Wayfinder, a Navigator Spirit, or suicidal luck.
- **The Discovery Loop:** This is the realm of ancient First Captain ruins, slumbering leviathans, and forgotten magic. Expeditions venture here seeking power, often returning with either legendary wealth or not at all.

## Major Magical Phenomena

The Sundering Tide did not just break the land; it broke the weave of magic itself, creating permanent geographical hazards.

### The Aether Streams
Massive, invisible rivers of magical energy that flow beneath the ocean's surface. Ships equipped with Arcane engines (such as a Tier 3 Legendary Vessel) can lock onto an Aether Stream, allowing them to sail at impossible speeds against the wind. However, these streams occasionally surge, threatening to tear apart hulls that aren't structurally reinforced.

### The Doldrums of Silence
Zones where the wind simply ceases to exist, and all sound is magically dampened. These regions can stretch for hundreds of miles. Ships caught in the Doldrums without magical propulsion or rowers will slowly starve to death. It is rumored that ghosts of the First Fleet haunt the deepest centers of the Silence.

### The Glass Storms
A localized weather event found near psionic ruins. Instead of rain, the clouds precipitate crystalline shards driven by gale-force winds. A Glass Storm can shred a mundane sail in minutes and severely damage a wooden hull. Navigators must either magically shield the ship or outrun the storm front.

## The Vertical Map: The Underdark Ocean
The Shattered Expanse is not limited to the surface. Beneath the waves lies the **Sunless Sea** (the Underdark Ocean). 
Accessible only through massive, whirlpool-like abysses known as "The Drown-Gates," the Sunless Sea is a subterranean ocean lit by bioluminescent fungi and magma vents. It is home to deep-dwelling empires, lost aberrations, and the most dangerous corsairs who use the subterranean waters to bypass surface blockades.

> [!NOTE]
> **DM Guidance on Geography:** The Shattered Expanse is designed to support the core campaign loop: Rumor -> Expedition -> Discovery -> Route -> Trade -> Conflict. By placing high-value ancient ruins in the dangerous Unwritten Sea, and the markets to sell those goods in the Charted Waters, you naturally force the players into conflict with both the environment and the rival factions!
