# Chapter 45: The Coral Citadel (Spirit Keepers Sanctuary)

*A sacred, living reef that breaches the surface of the water, cultivated over centuries by druidic magic.*

The Spirit Keepers do not build; they grow. The Coral Citadel is a massive atoll formed of vibrant, impossibly tall coral formations that reach hundreds of feet into the air. It is a sanctuary for aquatic life and a stronghold against the industrial expansion of the Dominion.

### Physical Description
The Citadel is breathtakingly beautiful. Waterfalls cascade down massive tiers of pink and blue coral. Giant glowing anemones provide light, and the "buildings" are naturally formed hollows within the reef. The air smells of salt, blooming sea-flowers, and ancient magic.

### Key NPCs
- **High Elder Naia:** The spiritual leader of the Keepers, deeply connected to the life-force of the Expanse. (See Chapter 37 for statblock).
- **Kaelen the Reef Warden:** A militant druid who commands the Citadel's defenses and believes the Dominion must be wiped out entirely to save the oceans.

### Local Laws & Dangers
- **The Sacred Edict:** No mechanical engines or Aether-tech are allowed near the Citadel. Any ship powered by an engine that approaches within 5 miles will be attacked by summoned sea life.
- **The Living Defenses:** The Citadel is alive. If the coral is damaged, it bleeds a highly acidic sap. Any creature dealing physical damage to the architecture takes 2d6 acid damage in return.

### Points of Interest

#### 1. The Elder Spawning Pools
Deep, warm pools at the center of the atoll where rare, magical aquatic beasts come to lay their eggs. It is a place of immense restorative magic. Any creature that takes a long rest here removes all exhaustion and curses. *Adventure Hook: The pools have been corrupted by a strange magical blight, and the players must dive deep to find the source.*

#### 2. The Whispering Kelp Forest
A dense underwater forest surrounding the base of the Citadel. The kelp is sentient and stores the memories of the Keepers' ancestors. DMs can use this area to provide lore drops or cryptic prophecies to the players.

#### 3. The Sunken Shrine of the Deep Mother
A terrifying, abyssal drop-off at the edge of the reef. The Keepers leave offerings here to appease the slumbering Leviathans. It is heavily guarded, and non-Keepers are strictly forbidden from approaching.
