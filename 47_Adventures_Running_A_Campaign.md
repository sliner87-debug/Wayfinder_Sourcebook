# Chapter 47: Running a Campaign in the Expanse

Running a game in the Shattered Expanse is fundamentally different from a standard high-fantasy dungeon crawl. The environment is actively hostile, the sea is an ever-present danger, and the factions are locked in a ruthless cold war. This chapter provides guidance for Dungeon Masters to set the tone and structure their campaign.

## Session Zero: Setting Expectations
Before the dice roll, DMs must establish the tone of the campaign with the players. The Shattered Expanse is a world of nautical horror, magic-punk industry, and piracy.

- **Morality:** The Expanse has very few purely "good" factions. Are the players acting as ruthless pirates out for themselves, honorable corsairs fighting the oppressive Dominion, or greedy Consortium mercenaries?
- **Naval Mechanics:** Ensure the players understand that they will be managing a ship. They must agree on a chain of command (who is the Captain, the Quartermaster, the Helmsman?) to avoid arguing during naval combat.
- **Lethality:** The sea is unforgiving. If a ship sinks in the deep ocean, it is often a total party kill (TPK). Players must understand that fleeing from a superior Dreadnought is often the only smart option.

## The Party's Origin: Starting Hooks
Why are the players on a ship together? Choose or roll on the following table to establish the party's starting bond:

| d6 | Origin Hook |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1** | **Mutineers:** The players were all press-ganged into serving on a Dominion frigate. They staged a bloody mutiny, took the ship, and are now on the run from Inquisitors. |
| **2** | **Consortium Debtors:** The players owe a massive, unpayable debt to the Vance Brothers. They have been given a junker ship and told to run highly illegal, dangerous errands until the debt is paid. |
| **3** | **Treasure Hunters:** A dying pirate gave the party half of a map to a sunken Aethelgard ruin. They pooled their money to buy a skiff and are setting out to strike it rich. |
| **4** | **Shipwreck Survivors:** The party's previous ship was destroyed by a Leviathan. They are the only survivors, washing up on the docks of Shipwreck Cove with nothing but the clothes on their backs. |
| **5** | **Keeper Envoys:** The party was hired (or commanded) by High Elder Naia to map the shifting magical currents and report back on Dominion fleet movements. |
| **6** | **The Inheritors:** One of the players inherited a heavily modified, but cursed, ship from a famous Free Captain relative. The ship comes with loyal crewmen and a lot of enemies. |

## Structuring the Campaign
A naval campaign usually follows a "Hub and Spoke" model. 
1. **The Hub:** The players start at a safe port (like Shipwreck Cove or the Golden Flotilla) where they rest, upgrade their ship, and gather rumors.
2. **The Voyage:** The travel is part of the adventure. Roll for random weather (storms, dead calms) and random encounters (Dominion patrols, sea monsters).
3. **The Spoke (The Location):** The players arrive at their destination (a precursor ruin, an enemy ship, or a faction island), complete the objective, and load up the plunder.
4. **The Return:** Surviving the journey back to port with a hull full of heavy, valuable loot is often the hardest part of the quest.
