# Chapter 29: The Expanse Arsenal

The Shattered Expanse is littered with the technological remnants of the First Captains, the aether-infused weaponry of the Dominion, and the cursed artifacts of the deep ocean. The items presented in this chapter are unique to the Charted Waters and can be used as high-value salvage, faction rewards, or black-market purchases.

---

## 1. Dominion Tech
*Forged in the industrial foundries of the Crimson Dominion, these items prioritize lethal efficiency, absolute order, and aether-combustion.*

#### Aether-Rifle
*Weapon (firearm), rare (requires attunement)*
**Cost:** 2,500 gp
This heavy, brass-and-iron rifle hums with volatile energy. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.
It fires concentrated bolts of force rather than physical bullets.
- **Damage:** 2d8 force damage.
- **Properties:** Ammunition (range 100/400), two-handed, reload (6 shots).
- **Overcharge:** As a swift action, you can overcharge the rifle for your next attack. If the attack hits, it deals an additional 2d8 force damage, but the weapon immediately jams. You must spend a standard action to vent the aether-steam before the weapon can be fired again.

#### Inquisitor's Monocle
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*
**Cost:** 800 gp
A brass monocle fitted with a lens carved from raw aether-crystal. While wearing this monocle, you have a +4 bonus on Sense Motive checks to determine if someone is lying.
Additionally, the monocle holds 1 charge. You can expend the charge to cast the *zone of truth* spell (save DC 13). The monocle regains its expended charge daily at dawn.

#### Ironclad Plating
*Armor (half plate), very rare (requires attunement by a creature with a Strength score of 15 or higher)*
**Cost:** 12,000 gp
This heavy suit of mechanized armor is powered by a small aether-engine mounted on the back. While wearing this armor, you gain a +1 bonus to AC.
- **Juggernaut:** Any critical hit against you becomes a normal hit.
- **Heavy Machinery:** The armor is incredibly loud and unwieldy. You take a -4 penalty on Stealth and Swim checks. If you fall into water while wearing this armor, you sink at a rate of 30 feet per round.

## 2. Consortium Artifacts
*Created by the Guildmasters of the Grand Merchant Consortium, these items are designed for securing wealth, enforcing contracts, and moving illicit goods.*

#### The Golden Ledger
*Wondrous item, rare*
**Cost:** 5,000 gp (Rarely sold, highly illegal to steal)
A heavy book bound in gilded leather. If a creature voluntarily signs their name in the ledger, agreeing to a specific debt or contract, the terms become magically binding.
If the signer breaks the terms of the contract, the ledger glows red. The oathbreaker is immediately subjected to a curse: they suffer a -4 penalty on all Charisma checks, and any gold or gems they touch instantly turn into worthless lead. The curse can only be lifted by the owner of the ledger crossing out the signature, or by a *remove curse* spell cast at 5th level or higher.

#### Smuggler's Coin
*Wondrous item, uncommon*
**Cost:** 500 gp
This silver coin bears the face of a laughing skull on one side and a blank surface on the other. As an action, you can flip the coin. When you do, a thick cloud of magical, obscuring fog erupts from the coin, heavily obscuring a 10-foot radius centered on you.
Simultaneously, you become *invisible* (as the spell) for 1 minute, or until you attack or cast a spell. The fog lasts for 1 minute or until dispersed by a strong wind. Once used, the coin becomes a mundane piece of silver.

#### Guildmaster's Signet
*Ring, very rare (requires attunement)*
**Cost:** 15,000 gp
A heavy gold ring bearing the seal of the Consortium. While wearing this ring, your words carry the supernatural weight of absolute authority.
- **Appraisal:** You can use an action to touch an object and instantly know its exact monetary value in the current local market, as well as identify any magical properties it possesses (as the *identify* spell).
- **Compel the Market:** The ring has 3 charges. You can expend 1 charge as an action to cast the *suggestion* spell (save DC 16). The ring regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

## 3. Deep Magic
*Salvaged from the Drowned Silk Houses or the abyssal trenches, these items carry the terrifying cold and predatory nature of the deep ocean.*

#### Black Coral Dagger
*Weapon (dagger), rare (requires attunement)*
**Cost:** 3,000 gp
Carved from a single piece of razor-sharp, obsidian-black coral, this blade seems to drink the light around it. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.
When you hit a creature with this dagger, the target takes an extra 1d6 cold damage. Furthermore, the freezing necrotic energy prevents healing; the target cannot regain hit points until the start of your next turn.

#### Abyssal Lure
*Wondrous item, uncommon*
**Cost:** 600 gp
A necklace bearing a small, bioluminescent orb harvested from a deep-sea predator. As an action, you can cause the orb to flare with hypnotic blue light.
One beast or monstrosity of your choice that you can see within 30 feet of you must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or become charmed by you for 1 minute. While charmed in this way, the creature is incapacitated and uses all its movement on its turn to move as close to the Lure as possible. The effect ends early if the creature takes any damage. Once used, the Lure cannot be used again until the next midnight.

#### Cloak of the Deep Mother
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*
**Cost:** 18,000 gp
Woven from the silk of abyssal spiders and enchanted by the High Priestesses of the Silk Houses. While wearing this cloak, you can breathe underwater, and you gain a swimming speed of 40 feet.
- **Ink Cloud Escape:** When you take damage, you can use your reaction to magically transform into a cloud of thick, black ink. You take no damage from the triggering attack or effect, and you instantly teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. Once you use this property, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

## 4. Relics of the First Captains
*These items are ancient, highly advanced technology that is often indistinguishable from magic. They are powerful, highly sought after by the Independent Salvagers, and occasionally unstable.*

#### Aegis Shield-Bracer
*Armor (shield), rare (requires attunement)*
**Cost:** 4,500 gp
A sleek, metallic bracer that hums with energy. While wearing this bracer, you can use a swift action to activate it, causing a disc of crackling blue "hard-light" to project from your wrist. While active, it provides a +2 bonus to AC, leaving your hand free to hold (but not attack with) other items.
- **Unstable Matrix:** When a creature rolls a natural 20 on a melee attack against you while the shield is active, the matrix overloads. You and the attacker both take 3d6 force damage, and the shield deactivates and cannot be used again until you finish a short rest.

#### Gravity Anchor
*Wondrous item, very rare*
**Cost:** 20,000 gp
A heavy cube of polished white metal, covered in shifting geometric lines. As an action, you can press the activation rune and throw the cube to a point on the ground up to 60 feet away.
Upon landing, the cube alters the localized gravity field. For 1 minute, the gravity in a 20-foot-radius cylinder extending 40 feet upward from the cube is completely reversed (as the *reverse gravity* spell, save DC 16). The cube cannot be moved while active. Once used, the cube goes dormant and cannot be used again until 7 days have passed.

#### The First Compass
*Wondrous item, legendary (requires attunement)*
**Cost:** Priceless
A perfectly spherical compass made of an unknown, indestructible glass. The needle inside does not point north. Instead, while you hold it, the needle points toward the object, creature, or location you desire most in the world.
- **The True Path:** The compass always shows the most direct route, but not the safest. If the path intersects with a physical barrier (like a mountain or the bottom of the ocean), the needle will point directly through it.
- **Aetheric Resonance:** The compass is highly radioactive with ancient magic. For every 24 hours you remain attuned to it, you must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or your hit point maximum is reduced by 1d10. This reduction lasts until you end your attunement to the compass and cast a *greater restoration* spell on yourself.
