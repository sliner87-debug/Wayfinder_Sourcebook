# Chapter 50: The Leviathan's Wake (Levels 7+ Campaign Arc)

*This chapter provides a high-level campaign framework rather than a room-by-room module. It is designed to act as the climax to a Shattered Expanse campaign.*

## The Premise
The delicate balance of the Shattered Expanse is broken. Deep within the abyssal trenches, a **Slumbering Leviathan** (CR 25 Titan, Chapter 36) has awoken. The massive creature is slowly swimming toward the surface, leaving a wake of destruction, tsunamis, and displaced sea monsters in its path. Its target: a major capital city (choose either Bastion, Shipwreck Cove, or the Golden Flotilla based on the players' alliances).

The players are the only ones with a ship fast enough, and the reputation necessary, to do something about it.

## Phase 1: The Warning
The campaign arc begins with unnatural phenomena. 
- Tides rise unpredictably, swallowing small islands. 
- Ships are found completely crushed, not by cannons, but by sheer physical force. 
- The players' ship is attacked by a panicked **Kraken** (CR 21) that is fleeing from the Leviathan's territory. The players don't have to kill the Kraken, just survive long enough to realize it is running *from* something bigger.

## Phase 2: Uniting (or Plundering) the Factions
To fight a CR 25 Titan, a single party of adventurers is not enough. They need a fleet.
The players must travel to the faction capitals to secure alliances.
- **The Crimson Dominion:** High Admiral Vane will only commit his Dreadnoughts if the players assassinate a rival Pirate Lord who is blocking their supply lines.
- **The Spirit Keepers:** High Elder Naia believes the Leviathan is a natural purge. The players must pass a difficult Skill Challenge (Diplomacy, Knowledge [religion], Knowledge [nature]) to convince her the beast is corrupted by Aether and must be stopped.
- **The Consortium:** The Vance Brothers will fund the war effort, but only if the players sign a *Golden Contract* promising them exclusive salvage rights to the Leviathan's corpse.

*Alternative Evil Path:* If the players are ruthless pirates, they can use the Leviathan's approach as a distraction. While the Dominion fleet sails out to fight the beast, the players can infiltrate Bastion and rob the Aether-Forges blind.

## Phase 3: The Weapon
Conventional cannons will only annoy the Leviathan. The players must seek out a mythical precursor weapon capable of stunning or killing the beast. 
- They must delve into a highly dangerous, uncharted Aethelgard ruin to retrieve **The Gorgon's Figurehead** (Chapter 39). 
- To retrieve it, they must bypass its guardian: an ancient **Ghost Ship Apparition** (CR 8) crewed by the cursed precursors who originally built the weapon.

## The Climax: The Battle of the Wake
The Leviathan breaches the surface near the targeted capital city. The sky turns black with aether-storms, and the water boils.
- The players command the flagship of the united fleet. 
- The DM runs a massive, multi-stage combat encounter.
- **Stage 1 (Naval):** The players must maneuver their ship to get within range of the Leviathan while avoiding its 300-foot *Tidal Wave* attacks. Allied faction ships provide covering fire, taking out the *Leviathan Spawn* that swarm the waters.
- **Stage 2 (The Figurehead):** The players must successfully activate the *Gorgon's Figurehead*, petrifying the beast's outer shell to make it vulnerable, taking the curse's penalty to their own ship.
- **Stage 3 (The Boarding Action):** The players must physically board the petrified-but-still-alive Leviathan. They fight their way across its back to shatter the glowing Aether-node on its spine, dealing the killing blow in traditional party-vs-boss combat.

## Epilogue
If the Leviathan is defeated, the players are hailed as the saviors (or the new undisputed rulers) of the Shattered Expanse. Their ship becomes legendary, and the factions must rebuild in the wake of the destruction, setting the stage for a new era on the high seas.
