# Chapter 2: Starting Origins

Every Wayfinder campaign begins with a shipwreck. The party does not start with a ship; they start with survival. 

## The Tide's Choice
There are two opening methods to determine where and how the players begin their adventure.

### Method A: The Captain's Choice
*“I chose where I wanted to go. The sea decided how I arrived.”*

The player chooses their **Home Port** and starting faction environment. 
The sea (via random dice rolls or GM selection) determines:
- Shipwreck Cause
- Rescue Vessel
- Rescue Circumstances
- Initial Reputation

### Method B: The Sea's Choice
*“I surrendered my destination, but I wrote my legend.”*

The player rolls randomly for their **Home Port**, letting fate decide their starting faction, local allies, and initial enemies. 
In return, the player chooses:
- Shipwreck Cause
- Rescue Vessel
- Arrival Circumstance

## Shipwreck Causes
The reason the ship went down shapes the player's initial mystery and backstory. Causes include:
- Leviathan attack
- Magical storm / Psionic distortion
- Drow naval assault / Crimson Dominion experiment
- Pirate battle / Mutiny / Betrayal
- Ancient defense system activated
- Underdark Ocean current breach / Planar ocean anomaly
- Ship vanished and reappeared damaged

## Rescue Vessels & Circumstances
The rescuers do not necessarily know they are rescuing someone important. The ship that pulls you from the water determines your first relationship and faction tie.

### Rescuer Options
- **Blackwake Connection:** A pirate exploration vessel hunting for treasure.
- **Port Meridian Connection:** A merchant expedition charting routes.
- **Crimson Dominion Connection:** A slaver/arcane vessel that recognizes something unusual about your survival.
- **Drow Connection:** Drow scouts drifting near an Underdark Ocean route.
- **Spirit Keeper Connection:** A vessel guided by spirits, deliberately looking for you.
- **Salvager Connection:** Wreck hunters where you are just another survivor.

### Arrival Circumstances
How you are treated on the rescue ship heavily influences your arrival at the Home Port:
- **Honored Guest:** Treated well.
- **Prisoner:** Rescued, but questioned and held under suspicion.
- **Debt Owed:** Saved, but expecting repayment.
- **Mystery Passenger:** The crew does not understand why you survived.
- **Prophecy/Recognition:** Someone on the ship knows exactly what you are.
