# Chapter 26: Custom Player Options

The Shattered Expanse is a world defined by the sea, ancient magic, and cutthroat factions. While standard backgrounds and class archetypes can be adapted, the options presented in this chapter are designed to root your character deeply into the lore of the Charted Waters. 

---

## 1. Setting-Specific Backgrounds

### Dominion Legionnaire
You were a soldier, inquisitor, or logistics officer for the Crimson Dominion. You are used to strict hierarchy, ironclad law, and the belief that order must be imposed on a chaotic world. You may still be active duty, or you may be a deserter fleeing the Iron Arbiter's wrath.

- **Skill Proficiencies:** Intimidation, Athletics
- **Tool Proficiencies:** One type of gaming set, land vehicles
- **Equipment:** A legionnaire's uniform, an insignia of rank, a set of bone dice, and a pouch containing 15 gp.

#### Feature: Military Bureaucracy
You know how to navigate the complex checkpoints and red tape of the Crimson Dominion. You can often secure an audience with lower-to-mid-tier Dominion officers, and you can comfortably requisition basic, non-magical supplies (rations, standard ammunition, simple weapons) from a Dominion garrison, provided you are in good standing.

Legionnaires are shaped by discipline and dogma. They often view the world in stark black and white, though some harbor secret doubts.

## 2. New Subclasses

### Bard: College of the Shanty
Bards of the College of the Shanty are not delicate lute-strummers playing in royal courts. They are loud, rhythmic leaders who use booming voices and percussive instruments to keep a crew working in perfect synchronization. Their magic is woven into the beat of the drum and the stomp of the boot, capable of turning a disorganized mob into a unified, terrifying force.

**Bonus Proficiencies**
When you join the College of the Shanty at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with water vehicles and one type of artisan's tools (such as carpenter's tools or cook's utensils). If you are already proficient, you can choose another tool proficiency.

**Heave Away**
At 3rd level, you can use your Bardic Inspiration to coordinate your allies. When an ally rolls a Bardic Inspiration die you gave them, they gain temporary hit points equal to the number rolled + your Charisma modifier. Furthermore, if they use the die to make a melee weapon attack, they can push the target 5 feet away from them on a hit.

**Voice of the Captain**
Also at 3rd level, your voice has been trained to carry over the roar of a hurricane. You can cast the *thaumaturgy* cantrip to triple the volume of your voice without providing verbal components. When you do so, you have advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks for the next minute.

**Rhythm of the Sea**
At 6th level, your voice cuts through the chaos of battle. As an action, you can begin a rhythmic chant. For 1 minute, as long as you maintain concentration (as if concentrating on a spell), you and friendly creatures within 30 feet of you who can hear you ignore difficult terrain and have advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.

**Thunderous Chorus**
At 14th level, you can unleash the accumulated energy of your crew's synchronization. When a creature within 30 feet of you deals damage to a target while under the effect of your *Rhythm of the Sea*, you can use your reaction to expend one use of your Bardic Inspiration. Roll the die; the target takes additional thunder damage equal to two rolls of your Bardic Inspiration die, and must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your spell save DC or be knocked prone.

### Barbarian: Path of the Leviathan
Barbarians who walk the Path of the Leviathan emulate the terrifying, crushing power of the ancient sea beasts that slumber beneath the waves. They do not just channel anger; they channel the abyssal pressure, the freezing cold, and the unstoppable mass of the deep ocean.

**Abyssal Adaptation**
Starting when you choose this path at 3rd level, you gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed, and you can breathe underwater. Additionally, you ignore any drawbacks caused by a deep underwater environment, including cold and pressure.

**Crushing Pressure**
At 3rd level, your rage manifests as a field of heavy, aquatic pressure. While you are raging, the ground within 10 feet of you is difficult terrain for your enemies. If an enemy starts its turn within this area, its speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of its next turn.

**Leviathan's Mass**
At 6th level, you can channel the unstoppable momentum of a sea beast. While raging, you have advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks made to grapple or shove a creature. Additionally, you count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift. Your melee weapon attacks deal an extra 1d4 bludgeoning damage while you are raging, representing the sheer weight of your blows.

**Depths of Despair**
At 10th level, you can exude the terrifying, cold isolation of the sunless sea. As an action, you can force all creatures of your choice within 30 feet of you to make a Wisdom saving throw (DC equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier). On a failed save, a creature is frightened of you for 1 minute. A frightened creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. Once you use this feature, you cannot do so again until you finish a long rest.

**Tsunami Strike**
At 14th level, when you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack while raging, you can unleash a shockwave of force. The target and all enemies within 15 feet of it in a line behind it must make a Strength saving throw (DC equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier). On a failed save, they take 4d6 bludgeoning damage and are knocked prone. On a successful save, they take half damage and are not knocked prone. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

### Rogue: Deep Diver
Rogues of the Deep Diver archetype are masters of 3D combat, aquatic infiltration, and scavenging. Often employed by the Free Captains or the Independent Salvagers, they use specialized gear and incredible breath control to strike from the darkest waters, exploiting the verticality of ship combat and flooded ruins to devastating effect.

**Aquatic Infiltrator**
When you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed. You can hold your breath for a number of minutes equal to 1 + your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1 minute) multiplied by 10. Additionally, you gain proficiency with tinker's tools if you don't already have it.

**Plunge Strike**
Also at 3rd level, you learn to use gravity and water to maximize your lethal precision. You can apply your Sneak Attack damage to a creature if you fall or swim at least 10 feet directly toward the target immediately before making the attack, even if you don't have advantage on the attack roll, provided you don't have disadvantage on it. All other rules for Sneak Attack still apply.

**Scavenger's Mobility**
At 9th level, you have jury-rigged a specialized grappling hook or aether-thruster. As a bonus action, you can target a solid surface within 30 feet of you. You are instantly pulled to that surface. If you end this movement suspended in the air (like grappling a mast), you have advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks to climb or hold on. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Dexterity modifier (minimum of 1), and regain all expended uses on a short or long rest.

**Trench Stalker**
At 13th level, you are fully at home in the abyssal dark. You gain darkvision out to 60 feet. If you already have darkvision, its range increases by 30 feet. Furthermore, while you are underwater, lightly obscured areas (like dim light or floating kelp) count as heavily obscured for the purpose of you taking the Hide action.

**Abyssal Ambush**
At 17th level, your strikes are as sudden and devastating as a Leviathan breaching the surface. When you use your Plunge Strike feature, you can roll d8s instead of d6s for your Sneak Attack damage. If you score a critical hit with a Plunge Strike, the target is stunned until the end of its next turn.

## 3. Setting-Specific Feats

If your DM allows the use of feats, you may choose the following options, which represent unique adaptations to the Shattered Expanse.

### Sea Legs
You have spent so much time on the unsteady decks of ships that the rolling waves feel more natural than solid ground.
- Increase your Dexterity or Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You have advantage on saving throws and ability checks against being knocked prone.
- Climbing rigging or swimming does not cost you extra movement.
- You have advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks made to balance on narrow or moving surfaces.

### Aether-Scarred
You survived prolonged exposure to the chaotic, magical radiation of the Unwritten Sea, leaving you physically marked and magically altered.
- Increase your Constitution or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You gain resistance to either Force or Lightning damage (your choice when you take this feat).
- You learn the *thaumaturgy* cantrip. When you cast it, the effects often have a slightly mutated, unsettling, or oceanic flair (e.g., your voice sounds like grinding tectonic plates, or the flames you flicker turn bioluminescent blue). You can cast this cantrip using Constitution as your spellcasting ability.

### Ship-Breaker
You have trained to breach enemy hulls and destroy fortifications.
- Increase your Strength score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- Your melee weapon attacks deal double damage to objects, structures, and vehicles (including ships).
- When you score a critical hit against a creature wearing heavy armor, you shatter a piece of it; the creature's AC is reduced by 1 until the armor is repaired (minimum AC 10).

### Abyssal Caster
You have learned to weave the freezing, crushing power of the deep ocean into your magic.
- Whenever you cast a spell that deals fire, lightning, or force damage, you can choose to have it deal cold or bludgeoning damage instead (your choice).
- When you cast a spell that deals cold or bludgeoning damage, you can reroll any 1s on the damage dice, but you must use the new roll.

## 4. Setting-Specific Spells

The following spells represent the unique magic developed by the inhabitants of the Charted Waters.

#### Aetheric Wake
*1st-level evocation*
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** Self (30-foot line)
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Classes:** Sorcerer, Wizard, Druid

You sweep your hand, creating a surging wave of glowing, heavy aether-water. Each creature in a line 30 feet long and 5 feet wide originating from you must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 2d8 bludgeoning damage and is pushed 10 feet away from you. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn't pushed.
**At Higher Levels:** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 1st.

#### First Captain's Cipher
*2nd-level divination*
- **Casting Time:** 1 minute
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a piece of ancient technology)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes
- **Classes:** Artificer, Warlock, Wizard

You touch a piece of technology, a machine, or a lock, and your mind connects to the fragmented databanks of the First Captains. For the duration, you have advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) and Dexterity checks made to understand, operate, or disable the object. Additionally, you receive flashes of the object's history, learning its original purpose and the general emotional state of the last person to use it.

#### Crushing Depths
*4th-level transmutation*
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a heavy stone from the ocean floor)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
- **Classes:** Sorcerer, Warlock, Druid

You manipulate the atmospheric pressure in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range, simulating the abyssal depths. The area becomes difficult terrain. When a creature starts its turn in the area or enters the area for the first time on a turn, it must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 4d8 bludgeoning damage and is deafened for 1 minute. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage and isn't deafened. A creature in the area cannot cast spells that require verbal components unless it succeeds on a Constitution check against your spell save DC.
**At Higher Levels:** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 4th.
