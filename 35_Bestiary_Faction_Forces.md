# Chapter 35: Bestiary - Faction Forces

This chapter provides standard 5e-compatible statblocks for the foot soldiers, elites, and specialized forces of the five major factions operating in the Shattered Expanse. DMs can use these creatures for boarding actions, port city brawls, and dungeon crawls within faction strongholds.

---

## 1. The Crimson Dominion

The Dominion relies on heavily armored infantry, arcane-suppression, and relentless discipline.

### Dominion Marine
*Medium humanoid (any race), lawful evil*
- **Armor Class:** 16 (Chain Mail)
- **Hit Points:** 22 (4d8 + 4)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Challenge:** 1/2 (100 XP)

**STR:** 15 (+2) | **DEX:** 12 (+1) | **CON:** 13 (+1) | **INT:** 10 (+0) | **WIS:** 11 (+0) | **CHA:** 9 (-1)
- **Skills:** Athletics +4, Intimidation +1
- **Senses:** passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common, Dominion Standard

**Traits:**
- *Phalanx Formation:* The Marine has advantage on attack rolls and saving throws if at least one ally is within 5 feet of it and isn't incapacitated.

**Actions:**
- *Halberd:* Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d10 + 2) slashing damage.
- *Heavy Crossbow:* Ranged Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d10 + 1) piercing damage.

---

### Dominion Inquisitor
*Medium humanoid (any race), lawful evil*
- **Armor Class:** 15 (Breastplate)
- **Hit Points:** 52 (8d8 + 16)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Challenge:** 3 (700 XP)

**STR:** 11 (+0) | **DEX:** 12 (+1) | **CON:** 14 (+2) | **INT:** 16 (+3) | **WIS:** 14 (+2) | **CHA:** 12 (+1)
- **Skills:** Arcana +5, Investigation +5, Perception +4
- **Senses:** passive Perception 14

**Traits:**
- *Mage Slayer:* When a creature within 5 feet of the Inquisitor casts a spell, the Inquisitor can use its reaction to make a melee weapon attack against that creature.

**Actions:**
- *Multiattack:* The Inquisitor makes two melee attacks or uses Aether Blast twice.
- *Aether-Iron Rapier:* Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) force damage.
- *Aether Blast:* Ranged Spell Attack: +5 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) force damage.
- *Suppressing Field (Recharge 5-6):* The Inquisitor projects a 20-foot aura of antimagic for 1 minute. Spells cast within the aura require a DC 13 Constitution saving throw, failing on a botched roll.

---

### Aegis Automaton
*Large construct, unaligned*
- **Armor Class:** 18 (Natural Armor)
- **Hit Points:** 85 (10d10 + 30)
- **Speed:** 20 ft.
- **Challenge:** 5 (1,800 XP)

**STR:** 18 (+4) | **DEX:** 8 (-1) | **CON:** 16 (+3) | **INT:** 3 (-4) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 1 (-5)
- **Damage Immunities:** poison, psychic
- **Condition Immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned

**Traits:**
- *Immutable Form:* The automaton is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.
- *Aether-Shielding:* Any allied creature within 10 feet of the Automaton gains half-cover (+2 AC and Dexterity saves).

**Actions:**
- *Multiattack:* The automaton makes two slam attacks.
- *Slam:* Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

---

### Ironclad Juggernaut
*Medium humanoid (cyborg), lawful evil*
- **Armor Class:** 20 (Plate, Shield)
- **Hit Points:** 119 (14d8 + 56)
- **Speed:** 25 ft.
- **Challenge:** 8 (3,900 XP)

**STR:** 20 (+5) | **DEX:** 10 (+0) | **CON:** 18 (+4) | **INT:** 10 (+0) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 14 (+2)
- **Saving Throws:** Str +8, Con +7, Wis +4
- **Damage Resistances:** bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks

**Traits:**
- *Relentless Advance:* The Juggernaut ignores difficult terrain and cannot be knocked prone.

**Actions:**
- *Multiattack:* The Juggernaut makes three attacks with its Aether-Maul.
- *Aether-Maul:* Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) force damage.
- *Overclocked Ram (Recharge 5-6):* The Juggernaut moves up to its speed in a straight line. Any creature in its path must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

## 2. The Grand Merchant Consortium

The Consortium fights with deep pockets, utilizing expensive magic, mercenaries, and bribery.

### Gold Cloak Mercenary
*Medium humanoid (any race), lawful neutral*
- **Armor Class:** 15 (Studded Leather, Parry)
- **Hit Points:** 33 (6d8 + 6)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Challenge:** 1 (200 XP)

**STR:** 11 (+0) | **DEX:** 16 (+3) | **CON:** 12 (+1) | **INT:** 10 (+0) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 10 (+0)
- **Skills:** Perception +3, Insight +3

**Traits:**
- *Hazard Pay:* The Mercenary has advantage on saving throws against being frightened as long as they believe they will be paid.

**Actions:**
- *Multiattack:* The Mercenary makes two melee attacks.
- *Rapier:* Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage.

**Reactions:**
- *Parry:* The mercenary adds 2 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the mercenary must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.

---

### Ledger-Mage
*Medium humanoid (any race), neutral*
- **Armor Class:** 12 (15 with *Mage Armor*)
- **Hit Points:** 49 (11d8)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Challenge:** 4 (1,100 XP)

**STR:** 9 (-1) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 11 (+0) | **INT:** 17 (+3) | **WIS:** 14 (+2) | **CHA:** 15 (+2)
- **Skills:** History +5, Persuasion +4

**Traits:**
- *Spellcasting:* The Ledger-Mage is a 5th-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 13, +5 to hit with spell attacks). It has the following wizard spells prepared:
  - Cantrips: *fire bolt, light, mage hand, prestidigitation*
  - 1st level (4 slots): *mage armor, shield, magic missile, identify*
  - 2nd level (3 slots): *hold person, misty step, locate object*
  - 3rd level (2 slots): *counterspell, dispel magic*

**Actions:**
- *Coin Barrage:* Ranged Spell Attack: +5 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (4d6) bludgeoning damage. (The mage literally flings high-velocity enchanted gold coins).

---

### Consortium Repo-Man
*Medium humanoid (any race), lawful evil*
- **Armor Class:** 16 (Studded Leather)
- **Hit Points:** 78 (12d8 + 24)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **Challenge:** 6 (2,300 XP)

**STR:** 12 (+1) | **DEX:** 18 (+4) | **CON:** 14 (+2) | **INT:** 13 (+1) | **WIS:** 16 (+3) | **CHA:** 11 (+0)
- **Skills:** Stealth +7, Perception +6, Investigation +4
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16

**Traits:**
- *Sneak Attack (1/Turn):* The Repo-Man deals an extra 14 (4d6) damage when it hits a target with a weapon attack and has advantage on the attack roll, or when the target is within 5 feet of an ally.

**Actions:**
- *Multiattack:* The Repo-Man makes three attacks with its shortswords or throwing daggers.
- *Shortsword:* Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage.
- *Paralyzing Poison Dagger:* Ranged Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d4 + 4) piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 minute. While poisoned, the target is paralyzed.

## 3. The Drowned Silk Houses

The Drow utilize terrifying flesh-crafting, stealth, and undead thralls from the deep ocean.

### Abyssal Thrall
*Medium undead, neutral evil*
- **Armor Class:** 11 (Natural Armor)
- **Hit Points:** 22 (3d8 + 9)
- **Speed:** 20 ft., swim 30 ft.
- **Challenge:** 1/2 (100 XP)

**STR:** 14 (+2) | **DEX:** 8 (-1) | **CON:** 16 (+3) | **INT:** 3 (-4) | **WIS:** 8 (-1) | **CHA:** 5 (-3)
- **Damage Immunities:** poison
- **Condition Immunities:** poisoned, exhausted

**Traits:**
- *Undead Fortitude:* If damage reduces the thrall to 0 hit points, it must make a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 5 + the damage taken, unless the damage is radiant or from a critical hit. On a success, it drops to 1 hit point instead.

**Actions:**
- *Rotting Claw:* Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution save or be poisoned until the end of its next turn.

---

### Deep Weaver
*Medium humanoid (elf), neutral evil*
- **Armor Class:** 13 (16 with *Mage Armor*)
- **Hit Points:** 45 (10d8)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Challenge:** 3 (700 XP)

**STR:** 9 (-1) | **DEX:** 16 (+3) | **CON:** 11 (+0) | **INT:** 12 (+1) | **WIS:** 14 (+2) | **CHA:** 16 (+3)
- **Skills:** Medicine +4, Stealth +5

**Traits:**
- *Innate Spellcasting:* The Drow's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma. It can innately cast *dancing lights* at will, and *darkness* and *faerie fire* 1/day each.
- *Flesh-Crafting Magic:* The Weaver can cast *cure wounds* or *inflict wounds* as a 2nd level spell (3 slots).

**Actions:**
- *Multiattack:* The Weaver casts a cantrip or makes two Bone Splinter attacks.
- *Bone Splinter:* Ranged Spell Attack: +5 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) piercing damage.

---

### Arachna-Drider (Aquatic)
*Large monstrosity, chaotic evil*
- **Armor Class:** 17 (Natural Chitin)
- **Hit Points:** 102 (12d10 + 36)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., climb 30 ft., swim 40 ft.
- **Challenge:** 7 (2,900 XP)

**STR:** 16 (+3) | **DEX:** 16 (+3) | **CON:** 17 (+3) | **INT:** 13 (+1) | **WIS:** 14 (+2) | **CHA:** 12 (+1)
- **Skills:** Stealth +6, Perception +5

**Traits:**
- *Spider Climb:* The drider can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down, without needing an ability check.
- *Amphibious:* The drider can breathe air and water.

**Actions:**
- *Multiattack:* The drider makes three attacks, either with its Trident or its Chitin Claws.
- *Chitin Claw:* Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) slashing damage.
- *Web (Recharge 5-6):* Ranged Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, range 30/60 ft., one creature. *Hit:* The target is restrained by webbing. As an action, the restrained target can make a DC 14 Strength check, bursting the webbing on a success.

## 4. The Free Captains Alliance

Pirates fight dirty. They rely on mobility, overwhelming aggression, and volatile magic.

### Corsair Swashbuckler
*Medium humanoid (any race), chaotic neutral*
- **Armor Class:** 14 (Leather Armor)
- **Hit Points:** 27 (5d8 + 5)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Challenge:** 1 (200 XP)

**STR:** 10 (+0) | **DEX:** 16 (+3) | **CON:** 12 (+1) | **INT:** 10 (+0) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 14 (+2)
- **Skills:** Acrobatics +5, Athletics +2

**Traits:**
- *Sea Legs:* The Corsair has advantage on ability checks and saving throws to resist being knocked prone.

**Actions:**
- *Multiattack:* The Corsair makes two melee attacks: one with its scimitar and one with its dagger.
- *Scimitar:* Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.
- *Dagger:* Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage.

---

### Powder Mage
*Medium humanoid (any race), chaotic evil*
- **Armor Class:** 13 (Leather Armor)
- **Hit Points:** 44 (8d8 + 8)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Challenge:** 4 (1,100 XP)

**STR:** 10 (+0) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 12 (+1) | **INT:** 16 (+3) | **WIS:** 11 (+0) | **CHA:** 14 (+2)

**Traits:**
- *Volatile Magic:* Whenever the Powder Mage casts a spell that deals fire damage, they roll 1d6. On a 1, the spell backfires, dealing 1d6 fire damage to the Mage in addition to its normal effects.

**Actions:**
- *Dragon-Pistol:* Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, range 30/90 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d8 + 2) piercing damage.
- *Fireball (1/Day):* The Mage hurls a magically enriched powder keg. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere must make a DC 13 Dex save, taking 28 (8d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half on a success.

### Wayfinder Captain
*Medium humanoid (any race), chaotic neutral*
- **Armor Class:** 16 (Studded Leather)
- **Hit Points:** 84 (13d8 + 26)
- **Speed:** 35 ft.
- **Challenge:** 6 (2,300 XP)

**STR:** 12 (+1) | **DEX:** 18 (+4) | **CON:** 14 (+2) | **INT:** 14 (+2) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 16 (+3)
- **Skills:** Acrobatics +7, Intimidation +6, Water Vehicles +5

**Traits:**
- *Wayfinder Step (3/Day):* As a bonus action, the Captain can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space they can see.

## 5. The Spirit Keepers

The Keepers utilize druidic magic, symbiotic plant life, and control over elementals to crush invaders.

### Reef Warden
*Medium humanoid (any race), neutral*
- **Armor Class:** 14 (Hide Armor)
- **Hit Points:** 39 (6d8 + 12)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.
- **Challenge:** 2 (450 XP)

**STR:** 14 (+2) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 14 (+2) | **INT:** 10 (+0) | **WIS:** 16 (+3) | **CHA:** 10 (+0)
- **Skills:** Nature +4, Survival +5

**Traits:**
- *Spellcasting:* The Warden is a 3rd-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 13, +5 to hit with spell attacks). It has the following druid spells prepared:
  - Cantrips: *druidcraft, shillelagh, thorn whip*
  - 1st level (4 slots): *entangle, thunderwave, cure wounds*
  - 2nd level (2 slots): *spike growth, moonbeam*

**Actions:**
- *Coral Staff (with Shillelagh):* Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage.

---

### Bound Water Elemental
*Large elemental, neutral*
- **Armor Class:** 14 (Natural Armor)
- **Hit Points:** 114 (12d10 + 48)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., swim 90 ft.
- **Challenge:** 5 (1,800 XP)

**STR:** 18 (+4) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 18 (+4) | **INT:** 5 (-3) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 8 (-1)
- **Damage Resistances:** acid; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks

**Traits:**
- *Water Form:* The elemental can enter a hostile creature's space and stop there. It can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.

**Actions:**
- *Multiattack:* The elemental makes two slam attacks.
- *Slam:* Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
- *Whelm (Recharge 4-6):* Each creature in the elemental's space must make a DC 15 Strength saving throw. On a failure, a target takes 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage and is grappled (escape DC 14) and restrained.

### Sentient Kelp Mass
*Large plant, unaligned*
- **Armor Class:** 13 (Natural Armor)
- **Hit Points:** 75 (10d10 + 20)
- **Speed:** 10 ft., swim 30 ft.
- **Challenge:** 4 (1,100 XP)

**STR:** 16 (+3) | **DEX:** 10 (+0) | **CON:** 14 (+2) | **INT:** 1 (-5) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 1 (-5)
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** fire
- **Damage Resistances:** bludgeoning, piercing

**Traits:**
- *False Appearance:* While the mass remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from normal kelp.

**Actions:**
- *Multiattack:* The kelp makes two constrict attacks.
- *Constrict:* Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 13). Until this grapple ends, the target is restrained, and the kelp can't constrict another target with that vine.
