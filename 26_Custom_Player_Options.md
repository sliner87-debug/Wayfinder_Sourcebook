# Chapter 26: Custom Player Options

The Shattered Expanse is a world defined by the sea, ancient magic, and cutthroat factions. The options presented in this chapter are designed for the **Pathfinder 1st Edition** ruleset (and compatible with v3.5), rooting your character deeply into the mechanics and lore of the Charted Waters. 

---

## 1. Campaign Traits

During character creation, a player may select one Campaign Trait to represent their background in the Shattered Expanse.

**Dominion Legionnaire**
You were a soldier for the Crimson Dominion, used to strict hierarchy and ironclad law.
- **Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on Intimidate checks, and Intimidate is always a class skill for you. Additionally, you gain a +1 trait bonus to your CMD against trip and bull rush attempts.

**Consortium Smuggler**
You grew up dealing in the black markets of the Grand Merchant Consortium.
- **Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on Sleight of Hand checks and Bluff checks. One of these skills (your choice) becomes a class skill for you.

**Silk House Exile**
You survived the terrifying Drowned Silk Houses, carrying the trauma of the deep dark.
- **Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on Survival checks in underground or aquatic environments, and Survival becomes a class skill for you. You also gain low-light vision (or darkvision +10 ft. if you already possess it).

**Spirit Keeper Warden**
You revere the Slumbering Leviathans and protect the Verdant Expanse.
- **Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on Knowledge (Nature) checks, and it becomes a class skill for you. You can also perfectly predict the weather in your current biome for the next 24 hours.

**Independent Salvager**
You dive into the treacherous Unwritten Sea to retrieve artifacts of the First Captains.
- **Benefit:** You gain a +1 trait bonus on Disable Device checks and Knowledge (Engineering) checks, and one of these becomes a class skill for you. You also start with one random mundane precursor trinket.

## 2. New Class Archetypes

The following archetypes and class options are available to characters navigating the Shattered Expanse.

### Barbarian: Leviathan Totem (Archetype)
Barbarians who channel the terrifying, crushing power of the ancient sea beasts that slumber beneath the waves.

- **Abyssal Lungs (Ex):** At 3rd level, while raging, the barbarian can breathe underwater and ignores the pressure of deep aquatic environments. This ability replaces Trap Sense +1.
- **Crushing Pressure (Su):** At 6th level, the barbarian's rage manifests as a field of heavy pressure. Enemies within 10 feet of the barbarian treat the area as difficult terrain. This ability replaces Trap Sense +2.
- **Leviathan's Mass (Ex):** At 9th level, while raging, the barbarian gains a +2 bonus to their CMB for grapple and bull rush checks, and counts as one size category larger for the purpose of determining carrying capacity and CMD. This ability replaces Trap Sense +3.
- **Tsunami Strike (Ex):** At 12th level, once per rage as a standard action, the barbarian can make a single melee attack that sends out a shockwave. All enemies in a 30-foot line must make a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 barbarian level + Str modifier) or be knocked prone and take damage equal to the weapon's base damage. This ability replaces Trap Sense +4.

### Bard: Shantyman (Archetype)
Bards who use booming voices and percussive instruments to keep a crew working in perfect synchronization.

- **Heave Away (Su):** At 1st level, a Shantyman can use bardic performance to coordinate allies. Allies who can hear the Shantyman gain a +1 competence bonus on attack rolls and a +2 competence bonus on combat maneuver checks. This bonus increases by +1 at 5th, 11th, and 17th levels. This replaces Inspire Courage.
- **Voice of the Captain (Ex):** At 2nd level, a Shantyman’s voice cuts through hurricanes. The bard gains a bonus equal to half their class level on Intimidate checks and can be heard clearly up to half a mile away. This replaces Versatile Performance.
- **Rhythm of the Sea (Su):** At 8th level, the Shantyman can use a swift action to expend 2 rounds of bardic performance. For 1 minute, all allies within 30 feet ignore movement penalties from difficult terrain or slippery decks, and gain a +4 morale bonus on saving throws against fear and charm effects. This replaces Dirge of Doom.

### Cleric: Deep Domain
Clerics who worship the Deep Mother or the ocean itself wield the terrifying pressure of the abyss.

- **Granted Powers:** You can manipulate water and crush your foes.
  - *Crushing Touch (Sp):* As a standard action, you can make a melee touch attack against a foe. On a hit, the target takes 1d6 points of nonlethal bludgeoning damage + 1 for every two cleric levels you possess, and its movement speed is reduced by 10 feet for 1 round. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.
  - *Abyssal Resilience (Ex):* At 8th level, you gain a swim speed equal to your base land speed and can breathe underwater indefinitely. You gain cold resistance 10.
- **Domain Spells:** 1st—*hydraulic push*, 2nd—*slipstream*, 3rd—*water breathing*, 4th—*control water*, 5th—*geyser*, 6th—*fluid form*, 7th—*vortex*, 8th—*seamantle*, 9th—*tsunami*.

### Druid: Reef Warden (Archetype)
Fierce protectors of the Shattered Expanse's fragile underwater ecosystems.

- **Reef Bond (Ex):** A Reef Warden must choose a domain (such as Water or Weather) instead of an animal companion. 
- **Living Coral Armor (Su):** At 4th level, as a standard action, a Reef Warden can expend one daily use of Wild Shape to grow a jagged carapace of coral. The druid gains a +4 natural armor bonus and temporary hit points equal to their druid level. Any creature that strikes the druid with a non-reach melee weapon takes 1d4 piercing damage. This effect lasts for 10 minutes per druid level. This ability alters Wild Shape.
- **Symbiotic Grapple (Su):** At 9th level, when the druid casts a spell that entangles or restricts movement (like *entangle*), they can attempt a grapple combat maneuver check against one creature in the area, using their caster level + their Wisdom modifier as their CMB. If successful, the creature is grappled by spectral kelp vines. This ability replaces Venom Immunity.

### Fighter: Dominion Marine (Archetype)
The Crimson Dominion relies on disciplined, heavily armored shock-troops.

- **Aether-Tech Training (Ex):** At 1st level, the Marine gains Exotic Weapon Proficiency (Firearms) as a bonus feat. They suffer no penalties for firing a two-handed firearm while prone. This replaces the 1st-level fighter bonus feat.
- **Breach and Clear (Ex):** At 3rd level, the Marine gains a +2 bonus on initiative checks and a +2 bonus on damage rolls during the surprise round or the first round of combat against targets that are flat-footed. This replaces Armor Training 1.
- **Ironclad Advance (Ex):** At 5th level, the Marine gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC when wearing heavy armor, and a +1 bonus to CMD. These bonuses increase by +1 for every four levels beyond 5th. This replaces Weapon Training 1.
- **Relentless Assault (Ex):** At 9th level, as a swift action, the Marine can move up to half their speed. This movement does not provoke attacks of opportunity if the Marine ends their movement adjacent to a hostile creature. They can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + their Constitution modifier. This replaces Weapon Training 2.

### Monk: Way of the Shifting Tide (Archetype)
Martial artists who fight like the sea itself: calm one moment, a devastating wave the next.

- **Sea Legs (Ex):** At 1st level, the monk uses their Wisdom modifier instead of Acrobatics to keep their balance on a moving ship. They can stand up from prone as a swift action without provoking attacks of opportunity. This replaces Stunning Fist.
- **Tidal Redirect (Ex):** At 3rd level, when an enemy misses the monk with a melee attack, the monk can spend 1 ki point as an immediate action to attempt a reposition or trip combat maneuver against the attacker with a +2 bonus. This replaces Still Mind.
- **Water Whip (Su):** At 7th level, as a standard action, the monk can spend 2 ki points to strike an enemy within 30 feet with a whip of magical water. The monk makes a ranged touch attack. On a hit, the target takes bludgeoning damage equal to the monk's unarmed strike damage + their Wisdom modifier, and the monk can attempt a free pull combat maneuver. This replaces Wholeness of Body.

### Paladin: Oath of the Horizon (Oath)
Driven by a holy compulsion to map the Unwritten Sea and protect their crew from abyssal horrors.

- **Tenets:** Discover the unknown, guide your crew, and adapt to the sea.
- **Oath Spells:** 1st—*know direction (as a 1st-level spell)*, 2nd—*status*, 3rd—*water walk*, 4th—*freedom of movement*.
- **Guiding Light (Su):** At 4th level, as a standard action, the paladin can expend one use of Channel Positive Energy to summon a flare of divine light. For 1 minute, the paladin sheds bright light in a 30-foot radius. Allies within this light gain a sacred bonus on attack rolls equal to the paladin's Charisma modifier (minimum +1). This replaces the standard Channel Positive Energy feature.
- **Aura of the Navigator (Su):** At 8th level, the paladin and allies within 10 feet gain a +4 bonus on Survival checks to avoid becoming lost, and ignore penalties from natural difficult terrain (like deep snow or mud). This replaces Aura of Resolve.

### Ranger: Leviathan Hunter (Archetype)
Fearless sailors who wield massive harpoons to butcher colossal beasts.

- **Harpoon Mastery (Ex):** At 1st level, the ranger treats the harpoon as a martial weapon. When the ranger hits a creature with a thrown harpoon attached to a trailing rope, they can attempt a trip combat maneuver as a free action that does not provoke attacks of opportunity. This replaces Wild Empathy.
- **Colossus Slayer (Ex):** At 2nd level, a Leviathan Hunter gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls against creatures of Large size or larger. This bonus increases by +1 at 6th, 10th, 14th, and 18th levels. This replaces Combat Style Feat at 2nd level (the ranger still gains combat style feats at later levels).
- **Iron Nerve (Ex):** At 3rd level, the ranger gains a +4 bonus on saving throws against fear effects. This replaces Endurance.
- **Whaler's Evasion (Ex):** At 11th level, when targeted by a melee attack from a creature Large or larger, the ranger can spend an immediate action to gain a dodge bonus to AC equal to their Wisdom modifier against that attack. This replaces Quarry.

### Rogue: Consortium Smuggler (Archetype)
Masters of the black market, moving illicit goods and avoiding harbor inspections.

- **Black Market Contacts (Ex):** At 1st level, the smuggler adds half their rogue level (minimum 1) to Appraise and Knowledge (Local) checks related to illicit goods. They can find a fence in any settlement within 1d4 hours. This replaces Trapfinding.
- **Hidden Pockets (Ex):** At 2nd level, the smuggler gains a +4 bonus on Sleight of Hand checks made to conceal small items on their person. This replaces the rogue talent gained at 2nd level.
- **Silver Tongue (Ex):** At 3rd level, the smuggler can reroll a failed Bluff or Diplomacy check once per day, but must take the second result. This replaces Trap Sense +1.

### Rogue: Deep Diver (Archetype)
Specialized in aquatic infiltration, striking from the darkest waters.

- **Aquatic Infiltrator (Ex):** At 1st level, the rogue gains a swim speed equal to half their base land speed. They can hold their breath for a number of rounds equal to four times their Constitution score before risking drowning. This replaces Trapfinding.
- **Plunge Strike (Ex):** At 4th level, if the rogue falls or swims at least 10 feet directly toward a target immediately before making a melee attack, they are treated as flanking the target for the purpose of dealing Sneak Attack damage. This replaces the rogue talent gained at 4th level.
- **Abyssal Ambush (Ex):** At 8th level, the rogue gains darkvision out to 60 feet. While underwater, the rogue can use the Stealth skill to hide even while being observed, as long as they are in dim light or darkness. This replaces the rogue talent gained at 8th level.

### Sorcerer: Aether-Touched (Bloodline)
A living battery of raw, unstable precursor energy.

- **Class Skill:** Knowledge (Engineering).
- **Bonus Spells:** *magic missile* (3rd), *levitate* (5th), *lightning bolt* (7th), *resilient sphere* (9th), *telekinesis* (11th), *disintegrate* (13th), *reverse gravity* (15th), *telekinetic sphere* (17th), *meteor swarm* (19th).
- **Bonus Feats:** Combat Casting, Empower Spell, Point-Blank Shot, Precise Shot, Spell Focus (Evocation), Toughness.
- **Bloodline Arcana:** Whenever you cast a spell that deals damage, you can choose to change the damage type to force damage. If you do, the spell gains the chaotic descriptor, and you take 1 point of nonlethal damage per spell level.
- **Bloodline Powers:** 
  - *Aetheric Blast (Sp):* At 1st level, as a standard action, you can unleash a ray of green force targeting a foe within 30 feet as a ranged touch attack. It deals 1d6 points of force damage + 1 for every two sorcerer levels you possess. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier.
  - *Aetheric Resonance (Su):* At 3rd level, you gain a constant feather fall effect, and ignore difficult terrain caused by rubble or uneven stone.
  - *Overclocked Spells (Su):* At 9th level, you can increase the caster level of a spell you cast by +2. If you do, you must succeed on a Fortitude save (DC 15 + spell level) or become staggered for 1 round.

### Oracle: Sunken King (Mystery)
Your powers are drawn from a dormant AI or digitized ancient admiral at the bottom of the abyss.

- **Class Skills:** Knowledge (Engineering), Knowledge (History).
- **Bonus Spells:** *shield* (2nd), *shatter* (4th), *lightning bolt* (6th), *secure shelter* (8th), *wall of force* (10th), *chain lightning* (12th), *control construct* (14th), *iron body* (16th), *meteor swarm* (18th).
- **Revelations:**
  - *Aegis Shielding (Su):* As a swift action, you can conjure a hard-light shield, granting you a +4 armor bonus to AC. At 7th level, and every four levels thereafter, this bonus increases by +2. The shield lasts for 1 hour per oracle level or until dismissed.
  - *Technomantic Thrall (Sp):* You can cast *make whole* at will. At 7th level, you can animate a medium-sized pile of scrap metal as an *animate objects* spell, but it functions as a permanent construct thrall under your control (similar to a familiar) until destroyed.
  - *Override Protocol (Su):* You gain a +4 insight bonus on saving throws against mind-affecting effects. At 11th level, you become immune to charm effects.

### Wizard: Cartography (Arcane School)
Wizards who manipulate the geometry of space, bending distance and location.

- **Prohibited Schools:** You must select two opposed schools (often Necromancy and Enchantment).
- **Cartographer's Eye (Ex):** You gain a bonus equal to half your wizard level on Knowledge (Geography) and Survival checks to navigate. You always know which way is north.
- **Spatial Shift (Su):** As a swift action, you can magically teleport up to 10 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Intelligence modifier. This movement does not provoke attacks of opportunity.
- **Rewrite Geometry (Su):** At 8th level, as a standard action, you can force a creature within 30 feet to swap positions with you. An unwilling creature can make a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 wizard level + Int modifier) to negate the effect. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to your Intelligence modifier.

### Gunslinger: Aether-Corsair (Archetype)
The Shattered Expanse is fraught with danger, and the Aether-Corsair uses volatile precursor technology and flawless agility to dominate ship-to-ship boarding actions.

- **Aether-Gunsmith (Ex):** At 1st level, the Aether-Corsair gains a battered aether-pistol or aether-rifle (which targets touch AC in its first range increment but misfires on a 1-2). They gain the Gunsmithing feat, but their craft relies on precursor scrap rather than black powder. This alters Gunsmithing.
- **Corsair's Deeds:** An Aether-Corsair swaps out several standard deeds for those suited to the high seas.
  - *Boarding Leap (Ex):* At 1st level, as long as the corsair has at least 1 grit point, they gain a +4 bonus on Acrobatics checks made to jump or swing on ropes. By spending 1 grit point as a swift action, they can move up to 15 feet ignoring difficult terrain and without provoking attacks of opportunity. This replaces the *Deadeye* deed.
  - *Hull-Breaker (Ex):* At 3rd level, the corsair can spend 1 grit point when making a ranged attack against an object, vehicle, or ship. The attack ignores up to 10 points of the target's hardness and deals double damage on a hit. This replaces the *Pistol-Whip* deed.
  - *Deep-Sea Shot (Su):* At 7th level, as long as the corsair has at least 1 grit point, their firearm attacks are not penalized for being fired underwater, and the range increment is not reduced by the water. This replaces the *Dead Shot* deed.
- **Nimble Rigging (Ex):** At 2nd level, an Aether-Corsair gains a +1 dodge bonus to AC while wearing light or no armor. This bonus increases by +1 for every four levels beyond 2nd. Additionally, they gain a climb speed equal to half their base speed when climbing rigging, ropes, or ship hulls. This alters Nimble.

## 3. Setting-Specific Feats

**Sea Legs (Combat)**
You have spent so much time on unsteady decks that rolling waves feel natural.
- **Prerequisites:** Dex 13.
- **Benefit:** You gain a +2 bonus on Acrobatics and Climb checks. You do not suffer penalties on attack rolls or skill checks due to fighting on a moving ship or uneven ground.

**Aether-Scarred**
You survived exposure to chaotic, magical radiation.
- **Prerequisites:** Con 13.
- **Benefit:** You gain resistance 5 to force damage and lightning damage. Additionally, you gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against chaotic-aligned spells or effects.

**Ship-Breaker (Combat)**
You have trained to breach enemy hulls and destroy fortifications.
- **Prerequisites:** Str 15, Power Attack, base attack bonus +3.
- **Benefit:** When you make a melee attack against an object or a vehicle, you ignore up to 5 points of its hardness.

**Abyssal Caster**
You weave the freezing, crushing power of the deep ocean into your magic.
- **Prerequisites:** Ability to cast 2nd-level spells, Spell Focus (Evocation).
- **Benefit:** Whenever you cast an evocation spell that deals cold or bludgeoning damage, the DC to resist the spell increases by +1, and you gain a +2 bonus on caster level checks made to overcome spell resistance.

## 4. Setting-Specific Spells

**Aetheric Wake**
**School** evocation [force]; **Level** druid 1, sorcerer/wizard 1
**Casting Time** 1 standard action
**Components** V, S
**Range** 30 ft.
**Area** line-shaped burst
**Duration** instantaneous
**Saving Throw** Reflex half; **Spell Resistance** yes
You sweep your hand, creating a surging wave of glowing, heavy aether-water. Creatures in the area take 1d6 points of force damage per caster level (maximum 5d6) and are pushed 5 feet away from you for every 10 points of damage taken. A successful Reflex save halves the damage and negates the push.

**First Captain's Cipher**
**School** divination; **Level** sorcerer/wizard 2
**Casting Time** 1 standard action
**Components** V, S, M (a piece of ancient technology)
**Range** touch
**Target** one technological object touched
**Duration** concentration, up to 1 min./level
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no
You connect to the fragmented databanks of the First Captains. You gain a +10 insight bonus on Disable Device and Knowledge (Engineering) checks related to the touched object. You also receive a brief vision of the object's original purpose and the emotional state of its last user.

**Crushing Depths**
**School** transmutation [water]; **Level** druid 4, sorcerer/wizard 4
**Casting Time** 1 standard action
**Components** V, S, M (a heavy stone)
**Range** medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
**Area** 20-ft.-radius spread
**Duration** 1 round/level (D)
**Saving Throw** Fortitude partial; **Spell Resistance** yes
You manipulate the atmospheric pressure in the area, simulating the abyssal depths. The area becomes difficult terrain. Any creature entering the area or starting its turn there takes 4d6 points of bludgeoning damage and is deafened for 1 minute. A successful Fortitude save halves the damage and negates the deafened condition. Spellcasting in the area requires a concentration check (DC 15 + spell level).
