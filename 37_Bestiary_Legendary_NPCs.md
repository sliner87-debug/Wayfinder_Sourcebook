# Chapter 37: Bestiary - Legendary NPCs

This chapter provides high-level boss statblocks for the most powerful figures in the Shattered Expanse. These entities possess Legendary Actions, Lair Actions, and unique mechanics suited for campaign climaxes.

---

### High Admiral Vane (Crimson Dominion)
*Medium humanoid (human), lawful evil*
- **Armor Class:** 21 (+3 Plate Armor)
- **Hit Points:** 190 (20d8 + 100)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Challenge:** 18 (20,000 XP)

**STR:** 20 (+5) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 20 (+5) | **INT:** 18 (+4) | **WIS:** 16 (+3) | **CHA:** 20 (+5)
- **Saving Throws:** Str +11, Con +11, Cha +11
- **Damage Immunities:** force

**Traits:**
- *Legendary Resistance (3/Day):* If Vane fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
- *Aether-Core Heart:* Vane is sustained by a piece of raw Aether implanted in his chest. Any spell that targets him specifically has a 25% chance to be absorbed by his heart, healing him for 1d10 HP per level of the spell instead.

**Actions:**
- *Multiattack:* Vane makes three attacks with his *Aether-Blade*.
- *Aether-Blade (Longsword):* Melee Weapon Attack: +13 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (1d8 + 7) slashing damage plus 14 (4d6) force damage.
- *Iron Dictate (Recharge 5-6):* Vane issues a crushing command. Each enemy within 60 feet must make a DC 19 Charisma saving throw. On a failure, they are stunned until the end of their next turn and drop whatever they are holding.

**Legendary Actions (3/Turn):**
- *Attack:* Vane makes one Aether-Blade attack.
- *Tactical Reposition:* Vane commands an ally he can see to immediately move up to their speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
- *Aether Vent (Costs 2 Actions):* Vane expels magical energy. Every creature within 15 feet must succeed on a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw or take 21 (6d6) force damage and be pushed 10 feet away.

### Hurricane Jax (Free Captains Alliance)
*Medium humanoid (half-elf), chaotic neutral*
- **Armor Class:** 19 (Enchanted Duster Coat)
- **Hit Points:** 165 (22d8 + 66)
- **Speed:** 45 ft.
- **Challenge:** 17 (18,000 XP)

**STR:** 14 (+2) | **DEX:** 22 (+6) | **CON:** 16 (+3) | **INT:** 14 (+2) | **WIS:** 14 (+2) | **CHA:** 22 (+6)
- **Saving Throws:** Dex +12, Cha +12
- **Damage Immunities:** lightning, thunder

**Traits:**
- *Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*
- *Storm-Step:* Jax does not trigger attacks of opportunity when he moves out of a hostile creature's reach.
- *Evasion:* If Jax is subjected to an effect that allows a Dexterity saving throw for half damage, he takes no damage on a success, and half damage on a failure.

**Actions:**
- *Multiattack:* Jax makes four attacks with his *Storm-Sabers*.
- *Storm-Saber:* Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (1d8 + 6) slashing damage plus 10 (3d6) lightning damage.
- *Lightning Tether (Recharge 4-6):* Jax hurls a lasso of pure electricity at a target within 60 feet. The target must make a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, they take 45 (10d8) lightning damage and are pulled up to 30 feet directly toward Jax.

**Legendary Actions (3/Turn):**
- *Dash:* Jax moves up to his speed.
- *Saber Attack:* Jax makes one Storm-Saber attack.
- *Thunderous Mockery (Costs 2 Actions):* Jax hurls an insult infused with sonic magic. One creature within 60 feet must make a DC 20 Wisdom save. On a fail, they take 16 (3d10) psychic damage and have disadvantage on all attacks until the end of their next turn.

### Matron Viconia (Drowned Silk Houses)
*Medium humanoid (drow), neutral evil*
- **Armor Class:** 17 (Flesh-crafted Armor)
- **Hit Points:** 153 (18d8 + 72)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Challenge:** 16 (15,000 XP)

**STR:** 10 (+0) | **DEX:** 16 (+3) | **CON:** 18 (+4) | **INT:** 20 (+5) | **WIS:** 18 (+4) | **CHA:** 16 (+3)
- **Saving Throws:** Con +9, Int +10, Wis +9

**Traits:**
- *Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*
- *Master Flesh-Crafter:* Any undead or monstrosity allied with Viconia within 60 feet has advantage on all attack rolls and saving throws.

**Actions:**
- *Multiattack:* Viconia casts a spell, then uses *Warp Flesh*.
- *Warp Flesh:* Ranged Spell Attack: +10 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 27 (6d8) necrotic damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution save or have one of its limbs twisted, suffering disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks for 1 minute.
- *Spellcasting:* Viconia casts one of the following spells (save DC 18, +10 to hit):
  - At will: *ray of sickness, chill touch*
  - 3/day each: *blight, animate dead (cast as an action)*
  - 1/day each: *finger of death, cloudkill*

**Legendary Actions (3/Turn):**
- *Cast Cantrip:* Viconia casts a cantrip.
- *Warp Flesh:* Viconia uses her Warp Flesh attack.
- *Command Thralls (Costs 2 Actions):* All allied undead and monstrosities within 60 feet can immediately use their reaction to move up to their speed and make one melee attack.

### High Elder Naia (Spirit Keepers)
*Medium elemental, neutral*
- **Armor Class:** 16 (Barkskin)
- **Hit Points:** 170 (20d8 + 80)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., swim 60 ft.
- **Challenge:** 16 (15,000 XP)

**STR:** 14 (+2) | **DEX:** 16 (+3) | **CON:** 18 (+4) | **INT:** 16 (+3) | **WIS:** 22 (+6) | **CHA:** 18 (+4)
- **Saving Throws:** Con +9, Wis +11
- **Damage Resistances:** bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks

**Traits:**
- *Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*
- *One with the Expanse:* Naia constantly projects a 30-foot aura. The area is considered difficult terrain for enemies, and allies within the aura regenerate 10 hit points at the start of their turns.

**Actions:**
- *Multiattack:* Naia makes two attacks with *Tidal Surge*.
- *Tidal Surge:* Ranged Spell Attack: +11 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 22 (4d10) bludgeoning damage.
- *Nature's Wrath (Recharge 5-6):* Naia causes massive coral spikes to erupt from the ground/deck in a 30-foot radius around her. Each enemy must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw, taking 42 (12d6) piercing damage on a failure, or half as much on a success.

**Legendary Actions (3/Turn):**
- *Tidal Surge:* Naia uses her Tidal Surge attack.
- *Symbiotic Healing:* Naia touches one creature. They heal for 20 hit points.
- *Elemental Shift (Costs 2 Actions):* Naia instantly teleports to any body of water or living plant within 120 feet.

### The Vance Brothers (Grand Merchant Consortium)
*Medium humanoids (human), lawful neutral*
- **Armor Class:** 18 (Enchanted Suits)
- **Hit Points:** 135 each (18d8 + 54)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Challenge:** 15 (13,000 XP) - *Fought together as a pair*

**STR:** 12 (+1) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 16 (+3) | **INT:** 20 (+5) | **WIS:** 16 (+3) | **CHA:** 18 (+4)
- **Saving Throws:** Int +10, Cha +9

**Traits:**
- *Legendary Resistance (3/Day) - Shared between them.*
- *Twin Abjuration:* As long as both brothers are within 30 feet of each other, they both have resistance to all magical damage.
- *Shared Lifeblood:* If one brother is reduced to 0 hit points, the other brother instantly enters a rage, gaining advantage on all attacks and dealing double damage.

**Actions (Each Brother rolls initiative separately):**
- *Multiattack:* The brother makes two attacks with their *Golden Canes*.
- *Golden Cane:* Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) bludgeoning damage plus 14 (4d6) psychic damage.
- *Hostile Takeover (Recharge 5-6):* The brother targets one creature within 60 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 18 Charisma saving throw. On a failure, the brother steals one of the target's prepared spells (level 5 or lower) or a magic item they are wearing (it teleports to the brother's hand).

**Legendary Actions (3/Turn shared between the brothers):**
- *Cane Strike:* One brother makes a Golden Cane attack.
- *Bribe (Costs 2 Actions):* One brother throws a handful of cursed gold at a creature within 30 feet. The creature must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by the brothers until the end of its next turn.

### Sergeant Kael (Ally / Defector)
*Medium humanoid (human), neutral good*
- **Armor Class:** 18 (Scavenged Dominion Plate)
- **Hit Points:** 112 (15d8 + 45)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Challenge:** 9 (5,000 XP)

**STR:** 18 (+4) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 16 (+3) | **INT:** 12 (+1) | **WIS:** 14 (+2) | **CHA:** 10 (+0)
- **Skills:** Athletics +8, Survival +6

**Traits:**
- *Veteran Instincts:* Kael has advantage on initiative rolls and cannot be surprised.
- *Protect the Crew:* When an ally within 5 feet of Kael is hit by an attack, Kael can use his reaction to swap places with the ally, taking the damage instead.

**Actions:**
- *Multiattack:* Kael makes three attacks with his *Boarding Axe* or *Heavy Repeating Crossbow*.
- *Boarding Axe:* Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) slashing damage.
- *Heavy Repeating Crossbow:* Ranged Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d10 + 2) piercing damage.
- *Grapeshot Grenade (3/Day):* Kael throws an explosive 30 feet. Each creature in a 15-foot radius must make a DC 15 Dexterity save, taking 21 (6d6) fire damage on a failure, or half on a success.
