# Chapter 46: The Sunken Ruins of Aethelgard

*The treacherous, magic-warped ruins of the precursor civilization that caused the Shattered Expanse event.*

Before the world flooded, Aethelgard was a continent-spanning empire built on unchecked magical hubris. Their experiments tore the crust of the earth apart, sinking their cities beneath the waves. Today, these ruins are the most dangerous—and lucrative—adventure sites in the Expanse.

### Physical Description
The ruins vary from half-submerged spires jutting out of the ocean like broken teeth, to massive, hermetically sealed biodomes resting in the abyssal trenches. The architecture is sleek, white stone mixed with veins of raw Aether crystal. The geometry is often broken, with stairs leading nowhere and gravity behaving erratically.

### Local Dangers
- **Aether Radiation:** The ambient magic here is highly unstable. Every hour spent exploring a major ruin, characters must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, they suffer a random magical mutation (e.g., skin turns blue, hair falls out, gain vulnerability to force damage) that lasts until cured by *Greater Restoration*.
- **The Silent Guardians:** Aethelgard was defended by advanced constructs (use statblocks for Golems or Aegis Automatons) that are still active, relentlessly following millennium-old patrol routes.

### Points of Interest

#### 1. Unstable Aether Vents
Geysers of raw magical energy that erupt from the sea floor near the ruins. They deal massive force damage but are surrounded by highly valuable, crystallized Aether shards. Harvesting them is incredibly dangerous. *Adventure Hook: A Consortium merchant has hired the crew to harvest shards during an active eruption.*

#### 2. Leviathan Breeding Grounds
The lingering magical radiation of the ruins attracts Leviathan Spawn. The massive beasts use the hollowed-out skyscrapers of the old world as lairs and nests. Exploring these ruins often means playing a deadly game of cat-and-mouse with a monstrosity.

#### 3. The Trapped Vaults
Aethelgard's greatest treasures (and most terrible cursed artifacts) are locked in sealed vaults. These vaults are protected by complex magical puzzles, anti-magic fields, and lethal traps. Opening one often requires finding key-runes scattered across the Expanse.
