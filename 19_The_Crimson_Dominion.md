# Chapter 19: Regional Gazetteer - The Crimson Dominion

*“Magic is not a gift. It is a weapon. The ocean is not a frontier. It is a territory. Those who disagree will row our galleys until they understand.”* — High Admiral Vane

The Crimson Dominion is the most formidable, expansionist, and terrifying military power operating in the Charted Waters. They are a magocracy built upon a foundation of absolute subjugation, believing that those who wield arcane power possess the divine right to rule, and those who lack it exist solely to serve. 

## 1. Geography, Demographics & Hubs

The Dominion lays claim to the **Cinder Atolls**, a highly volcanic, resource-rich cluster of islands on the eastern edge of the Charted Waters. 

**Population Overview:**
- Total Estimated Population: ~2.5 Million
- The Arcane Admiralty & Elite: 5%
- The Iron Legion (Military) & Citizens: 35%
- The Bound (Enslaved Caste): 60%

### The Crimson Citadel (Capital Hub)
The capital is not merely a city; it is a fortress carved directly into the caldera of a dormant, massive volcano. The caldera is open to the sea, providing an impenetrable natural harbor.
- **The Obsidian Spire:** The central tower rising from the harbor water, housing the Arcane Admiralty. It glows with eternal, magical fire.
- **The Ash Markets:** The civilian and merchant districts, built in tiers along the interior walls of the volcano.
- **The Deep Pens:** The horrific slave quarters located near the magma vents, used for geothermal heating and psychological breaking.

### The Iron Shipyards
Located on a neighboring island stripped bare of all vegetation. Here, massive drydocks use bound fire and earth elementals to smelt steel and shape hull plating. The air is choked with smog, and the harbor is constantly churning with the launch of new warships.

### The Border Forts (The Crimson Line)
A perimeter of heavily fortified, magically warded island outposts. Any ship that crosses The Crimson Line without a Dominion writ is immediately fired upon by heavy arcane siege engines.

## 2. Magic Systems Integration

The Dominion’s approach to magic is entirely utilitarian and weaponized.

- **Arcane Magic (The Backbone):** Evocation and Abjuration are the highest-honored schools. Wizards are drafted into the military at a young age. Dominion ships use wands of *Fireball* mounted on gimbals instead of black powder cannons, and project massive *Wall of Force* shields to deflect incoming fire.
- **Psionics (Internal Control):** Psions form the Dominion Inquisition. They are used for long-range, secure fleet communication (bypassing the need for flags or couriers) and for thought-policing the slave populations and lower military ranks. 
- **Spirit Magic (Banned/Exploited):** The Dominion considers Spirit Magic primitive and dangerous. When they encounter elementals or nature spirits, they use Arcane binding circles to trap them, turning them into living batteries to power ship engines and forges.
- **Deep Magic (Feared & Ignored):** The Dominion's arrogance blinds them to the deep ocean. They view the Underdark Ocean as a myth or a wasteland, making their heavily armored surface fleets highly vulnerable to attacks from below.

## 3. Faction Relations (Geopolitical Matrix)

The Dominion operates on a policy of eventual total conquest. 

- **Free Captains Alliance:** *Active Extermination.* The Dominion views pirates as vermin disrupting their divine right to rule the ocean. They hang captured Wayfinders from their rigging as warnings.
- **Grand Merchant Consortium:** *Cold War / Exploitative Trade.* The Dominion relies on the Consortium for rare spell components and raw materials. They tolerate them—for now—but actively plant spies in Port Meridian, planning for the day they simply annex the trade network.
- **Drowned Silk Houses (Drow):** *Shadow War.* A secret, brutal conflict rages. The Dominion loses ships to "mysterious storms" that are actually Drow submarine ambushes. The Admiralty officially denies the Drow exist to avoid looking weak.
- **Spirit Keepers:** *Hostile Exploitation.* The Dominion actively hunts the ancient leviathans the Spirit Keepers protect, seeking to harvest their blood and bone for magical experimentation.
- **Independent Salvagers:** *Regulated Tolerance.* The Dominion desperately wants First Captain technology. They allow the Salvagers to operate in their waters, provided the Dominion gets the right of first refusal on all magical artifacts pulled from the Unwritten Sea.

## 4. Societal Hierarchy (The Caste System)

Social mobility in the Dominion is impossible without magical aptitude.

1. **The Arcane Admiralty:** The absolute rulers. Consists of twelve High Mages who hold the rank of Admiral. They dictate law, military strategy, and magical research.
2. **The Magi-Officers:** Commissioned ship captains, tacticians, and court wizards. To hold rank on a ship, one must be capable of casting at least 3rd-level spells.
3. **The Iron Legion:** The non-magical standing army. Highly disciplined, heavily armored marines and city guards. They are respected, but ultimately viewed as expendable shields for the Magi.
4. **The Merchant Class:** State-approved civilians. Bureaucrats, quartermasters, and sanctioned traders who manage the empire's logistics.
5. **The Bound:** The slave caste. Comprised of captured pirates, conquered islanders, and political dissidents. They handle all physical labor, from mining to rowing the massive galleys when the wind fails.

## 5. Internal Politics & Rival Factions

The Dominion projects absolute unity, but beneath the surface, it is a nest of vipers.

- **The Traditionalists:** High mages who believe the empire should rely strictly on pure Arcane superiority and look down on those who meddle with foreign magic.
- **The Artificers Guild:** Radicals within the shipyards who want to integrate salvaged, unstable First Captain technology with modern ship designs, often resulting in catastrophic explosions.
- **The Inquisition:** The terrifying psionic secret police. They are supposed to hunt traitors, but they have grown so powerful that even Magi-Officers fear them.
- **The Liberation Front:** A highly secretive, violently suppressed abolitionist movement operating in the deep mines and ship bilges. They communicate via smuggled thieves' cant and are desperate for outside help (a perfect hook for a Wayfinder crew).

## 6. The Military Machine

The Crimson Armada is not merely a navy; it is an industrialized magical war machine. Their tactical doctrine relies on overwhelming arcane superiority, unbreakable discipline, and heavily armored vessels that laugh at traditional cannon fire.

### 6.1 Chain of Command & Ranks
The Dominion military strictly divides arcane casters from mundane soldiers, creating a stark caste system even aboard ship.
- **High Admiral (The Council of Twelve):** The supreme commanders of the Dominion, operating from the Obsidian Spire. They are exclusively Archmages of the Evocation and Abjuration schools.
- **Fleet-Magus (Admiral):** Commands a flotilla of 5-10 ships. Usually a Wizard of at least 13th level.
- **Spell-Captain (Captain):** The commanding officer of a single vessel. They wear crimson robes lined with steel pauldrons. They lead the ship's casting batteries.
- **Iron Praetor (First Mate/Marine Commander):** The highest-ranking non-magical officer. They do not steer the ship; they command the Iron Legion marines in boarding actions and enforce discipline with brutal efficiency.
- **Aether-Helmsman (Quartermaster):** Specialized artificers or sorcerers who interface directly with the ship's bound elementals, steering the vessel via magical conduits rather than a traditional wheel.
- **Legionnaire (Marine):** The rank-and-file of the Iron Legion. Heavily armored in plate, wielding halberds and heavy crossbows.

### 6.2 Standard Naval Doctrines
- **The Aegis Phalanx:** Dominion ships never engage alone. They travel in perfectly spaced diamond formations. If one ship is targeted by heavy fire, neighboring ships cast *Wall of Force* or *Otiluke's Resilient Sphere* to shield it.
- **The Arcane Volley:** Instead of relying on black powder (which is susceptible to wet powder and backfires), Dominion ships use batteries of low-level mages (Evokers). When the Spell-Captain drops their baton, the battery casts synchronized *Magic Missiles* or *Lightning Bolts* directly at the enemy's waterline.
- **The Iron Boarding:** The Iron Legion never swings on ropes. Dominion ships use heavy, magically-propelled grappling chains that forcefully drag the enemy vessel hull-to-hull. Magi then cast *Hold Person*, *Slow*, or *Web* onto the enemy deck, while the heavily armored Iron Legion marches across wide gangplanks to methodically slaughter the paralyzed crew.

### 6.3 Specific Ship Classes
- **The *Ignis*-Class Frigate (The Hunter):** Fast, sleek interceptors. They have no sails. Instead, they are propelled by a bound water elemental pushing a jet stream beneath the hull. Armed with forward-facing Arcane Ballistae. Used for hunting pirates and blockading ports.
- **The *Aegis*-Class Galleon (The Line-Breaker):** The standard ship of the line. Plated in Crimson Iron (granting fire resistance). They house a battery of 20 battle-mages and carry a compliment of 100 Iron Legion marines.
- **The *Obsidian*-Class Dreadnought (The Flagship):** Massive, floating fortresses propelled by bound fire elementals turning colossal paddlewheels. They carry a massive central arcane cannon (The God-Spear) capable of casting a maximized *Disintegrate* beam once per hour. Only three exist in the Armada.
- **The *Inquisitor*-Class Sub-Chaser (The Silent Eye):** Small, stealthy vessels painted pitch black. They use Psionic scrying nets dragged through the water to detect the brainwaves of submerged Drow submarines or hiding Wayfinders.

### 6.4 Magical Ship Integration
- **Elemental Cores:** The heart of a Dominion ship is its Engine Room, where bound elementals are tortured and siphoned for raw locomotive power. If the containment runes are broken in combat, the elemental breaks free, usually destroying the ship from the inside out in a violent rage.
- **Scrying Nets:** Dominion rigging is often interwoven with divination magic. Climbing the rigging of a Dominion ship while invisible will often trigger magical alarms on the bridge.
- **Warded Hulls:** The hulls are inscribed with Abjuration runes, giving them exceptional Hardness against magical attacks, but making them ironically vulnerable to raw, overwhelming physical force (like a Leviathan ramming them).

## 7. Notable Figures

- **High Admiral Vane:** The ruthless, calculating leader of the Arcane Admiralty. A master Evoker who lost an arm in a pirate raid and replaced it with a prosthetic forged of living magma. He seeks to completely eradicate the Free Captains.
- **Inquisitor Kaelen:** The terrifying head of the Dominion's psionic secret police. Kaelen is rarely seen, preferring to project his consciousness into the minds of his agents. He is currently obsessed with rooting out the Liberation Front.
- **Arbiter Zyla:** A highly decorated Magi-Officer who secretly sympathizes with the abolitionists. She commands the frigate *Iron Resolution* and occasionally turns a blind eye to smuggler vessels carrying escaped slaves.

## 8. Player Character Integration

- **Best Fit Backgrounds:** Soldier, Sage, Acolyte, Noble, Prisoner (The Bound).
- **Favored Classes:** Wizard (Evocation, Abjuration), Fighter (Eldritch Knight, Battle Master), Artificer (Artillerist). 
- **Roleplay Hook:** A Dominion PC might be a loyalist trying to prove their worth, an exiled mage who grew a conscience, or a former slave (The Bound) seeking bloody vengeance against their former masters.

## 9. Unique Regional Assets

- **Arcane Ballistae:** Siege weapons that don't use ammunition. They draw on the operator's spell slots (or a bound elemental core) to fire bolts of raw force damage.
- **Crimson Iron:** A dense, magically treated steel that grants Resistance to Fire damage. It is incredibly heavy, requiring specialized ship designs to stay buoyant.
- **Wands of the Line:** Standard-issue evocation wands given to Magi-Officers, which can be slotted into shipboard amplification arrays to cast extended-range *Fireballs* during naval combat.
