# Chapter 5a: Captain's Techniques (Rules & Framework)

*“Every captain leaves behind stories. The First Captains left behind techniques.”*

A Captain's Technique is not a spell, a maneuver, or a psionic power. It is a legendary trick, habit, doctrine, or philosophy that has become supernatural through repetition and authority. The Wayfinder learns 6 Captain's Techniques over their career (at levels 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18), though class features/milestones may grant more.

## Mechanics
- **Save DC Formula:** `10 + 1/2 Wayfinder level + Charisma Modifier`.
- **Usage Limits:** Unless stated otherwise, they are generally at-will or have specific per-encounter/per-day limits outlined in their text.
- **Action Types:** Standard 5e actions (Action, Bonus Action, Reaction, Special).
  - *Global Rule:* A Wayfinder may only use **one Reaction Captain's Technique per round** to prevent reaction bloat.

## Categories
Each category has a unique gameplay identity. If a technique solves a problem, the category defines *how* it solves it.
1. **Seamanship:** Environmental mastery. Navigation, ship handling, weather, avoiding hazards. "How do I move through the world?"
2. **Duelist:** Personal combat mastery. Precision, ripostes, officer challenges, exploiting openings. "How do I win this fight?"
3. **Corsair:** Aggressive raider. Boarding, fear, plunder, breaking defenses (siege). "How do I break the enemy?" *(Note: Corsair siege techniques create vulnerabilities, not automatic destruction).*
4. **Fleet:** Tactical commander. Coordination, formations, positioning. "How do we fight as one?" *(Note: A creature can benefit from only one Fleet command effect at a time).*
5. **Hunter:** Tracking and pursuit. Reconnaissance, interception, elimination. "How do I find what is hidden?"
6. **Mystic:** Keeper of sea secrets. Sea magic, omens, supernatural navigation. "What does the world reveal?" *(Note: Mystic info provides clues/probabilities, not perfect answers).*
7. **First Captain:** Sovereign commander. Leadership, territory, diplomacy, political authority. "How do I build influence?" *(Note: Creates leverage and reputation, not automatic obedience).*

## The Tiers
1. **Least (Levels 1-5):** *The Deckhand Becomes an Officer.* Focuses on personal skill, small tactical advantages, basic navigation. Scale: Self/1 Ally.
2. **Lesser (Levels 6-11):** *The Officer Becomes a Captain.* Focuses on crew coordination, resource efficiency, and boarding dominance. Scale: Small squad/Ship.
3. **Greater (Levels 12-17):** *The Captain Becomes an Admiral.* Encounter-defining commands, fleet tactics, environmental manipulation. Scale: Entire battlefield.
4. **Legendary (Levels 18-20):** *The Admiral Becomes the First Captain.* Maritime sovereignty, campaign-scale influence, world map changes. Scale: Regions/Factions/Campaigns. *(Note: Permanent world-changing effects require time, resources, and narrative consequences).*
