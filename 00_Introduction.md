# Introduction: Welcome to the Shattered Expanse

*The world did not end in fire. It ended in a flood of magic.*

Welcome to the **Wayfinder Sourcebook**, a comprehensive campaign setting and rules expansion for the Pathfinder Roleplaying Game (1st Edition). This book transforms your tabletop experience from traditional high-fantasy dungeon crawling into a high-stakes, nautical, magic-punk adventure. 

## The Shattered Expanse
Centuries ago, the precursor civilization of Aethelgard pushed magical industrialization too far. Their relentless harvesting of Aether—the raw, physical manifestation of magic—destabilized the earth's crust. Continents sank, oceans boiled, and the world was flooded. 

Today, the Shattered Expanse is an endless, violent ocean dotted with the volcanic peaks of drowned continents. It is a world where survival depends on the strength of your hull, the loyalty of your crew, and the firepower of your cannons.

## Themes of the Setting
When running a campaign in the Expanse, DMs and players should lean into the following themes:
1. **Nautical Magic-Punk:** Magic is not just whispered incantations; it is industry. Glowing blue Aether crystals power massive ironclad engines, replace lost limbs with mechanical prosthetics, and fuel devastating ship-to-ship weaponry.
2. **Deep-Sea Horror:** The depths are lightless and unforgiving. The magical radiation of the old world has twisted the local fauna into colossal Leviathans, and the darkness hides the insidious, flesh-warping Drow who rule the trenches.
3. **Ruthless Piracy & Politics:** There is no shining kingdom of good. The Crimson Dominion enforces its will through brutal inquisition, the Merchant Consortium values gold above human life, and the Free Captains are chaotic opportunists. Morality is a luxury on the high seas.

## How to Use This Book
This sourcebook is divided into five parts:
*   **Part I: Character Creation (Ch 1-10)** introduces the new Wayfinder class, thematic races, and the four unique magic systems (Aether-tech, Biomancy, Consortium Ledger-Magic, and Sea-Witchcraft).
*   **Part II: World & Lore (Ch 11-20)** details the history of the world and provides deep dives into the major factions.
*   **Part III: The Naval Rulebook (Ch 21-30)** contains robust, playtested rules for ship-to-ship combat, vessel upgrades, and specific Faction ship statblocks.
*   **Part IV: Threats & Treasures (Ch 31-40)** provides a massive bestiary of sea monsters, faction forces, and a vault of powerful magic items and cursed artifacts.
*   **Part V: The Gazetteer & Adventures (Ch 41-50)** maps out the major ports of call and provides four pre-written adventure modules to start your campaign immediately.

Hoist the sails, load the Aether-cannons, and prepare to chart the Unwritten Sea. The Expanse awaits.
