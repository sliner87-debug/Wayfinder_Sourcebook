# Chapter 42: Shipwreck Cove (The Neutral Haven)

*A sprawling, chaotic, floating shantytown made entirely from the lashed-together hulls of hundreds of sunken and captured ships.*

Shipwreck Cove is the beating heart of the Free Captains Alliance and the only truly "free" port in the Shattered Expanse. It has no fixed location; it drifts on the ocean currents, hidden from the Dominion by powerful, ship-wide illusion magic (similar to the *False-Flag Pennant*). It is a city of vice, freedom, and extreme danger.

### Physical Description
The city is a three-dimensional maze of rotting wood, canvas sails, and rope bridges. Galleons are stacked atop frigates, creating artificial "hills" of timber. The smell of cheap rum, black powder, and roasting fish is omnipresent. The city constantly groans and sways with the ocean swells.

### Key NPCs
- **Hurricane Jax:** The charismatic and deadly pirate king who acts as the unofficial mayor of the Cove. He mediates disputes between rival pirate crews. (See Chapter 37 for statblock).
- **"One-Eye" Pete:** The proprietor of the Powder-Keg Tavern and the best fence for stolen Consortium goods in the Expanse.

### Local Laws & Dangers
- **The Pirate's Code:** There is only one real law—do not betray the Cove to the Dominion or the Consortium. Those found guilty of treason are keelhauled beneath the city's massive, barnacle-encrusted underbelly.
- **Structural Instability:** The Cove is constantly shifting. During a fight, there is a 10% chance each round that the floor (a rotting deck or frayed rope bridge) collapses. Creatures must make a DC 15 Reflex save or fall 20 feet into the shark-infested waters below.

### Points of Interest

#### 1. The Powder-Keg Tavern
Built inside the overturned hull of a colossal Consortium Man-o-War, this is the largest tavern in the Expanse. It is a neutral ground where rival crews come to drink, gamble, and recruit. Violence is heavily discouraged here, enforced by Jax's elite bouncers. *Adventure Hook: A map to a legendary cursed artifact is being auctioned off in the back room tonight.*

#### 2. The Black Market Bazaars
A tangled web of rickety stalls selling everything from volatile Aether-dust to illegal biomancy implants and stolen Dominion weapons. If it's illegal anywhere else, it's sold here.

#### 3. The Corsair's Fighting Pits
Located in the flooded hold of a ruined galleon, pirates bet heavily on blood sports. Combatants fight on slippery, floating wooden platforms while captive sea monsters (like Abyssal Anglerfish) circle in the water below.
