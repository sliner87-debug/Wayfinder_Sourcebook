# Chapter 35: Introductory Adventure - The Wreck of the Aether-Tide

*The Wreck of the Aether-Tide* is a short, level 1 introductory adventure designed for 4-5 players. It is designed to teach the GM and the players the core mechanics of the Shattered Expanse setting: Naval Combat, Aether-Burn, and the looming faction war.

**Character Advancement:** Characters who complete this adventure should reach level 2.

## Background
A small Grand Merchant Consortium survey ship, the *Gilded Wake*, has run aground on a jagged, uncharted reef. However, this is no ordinary reef—it is a piece of precursor architecture (a "vault") that has suddenly surged from the ocean floor. The *Gilded Wake* is slowly sinking, but not before broadcasting an aether-distress signal. 

The players begin as the newly assembled crew of the *Salty Dog*, a barely seaworthy sloop. They intercept the signal. Unfortunately, so did a Free Captains pirate skiff and a Crimson Dominion patrol.

---

## Part 1: First Blood on the Water (Naval Combat Tutorial)

As the players' ship approaches the coordinates of the distress signal, the fog parts to reveal the jagged, black-metal spire of the precursor vault jutting from the waves. Impaled upon it is the *Gilded Wake*.

Before the players can dock and salvage the wreck, a Free Captains pirate skiff, the *Bloodfin*, intercepts them.

### Encounter: The *Bloodfin* Skiff
The *Bloodfin* is a tiny, fast ship crewed by three pirates who want the salvage for themselves. This is a tutorial for the **Naval Combat (Chapter 27)** rules.

**The Bloodfin (Skiff)**
- **Hull Points:** 30 (Hardness 5)
- **Speed:** 60 ft.
- **Armament:** 1 Light Ballista (3d8 piercing, Range 120 ft.)

**The Pirate Crew (x3)**
- **CR 1/2** (XP 200 each)
- Human Warrior 1
- **hp** 6 each; **AC** 13 (leather armor, +1 Dex)
- **Melee** Cutlass +2 (1d6+1/18-20)
- **Ranged** Light Crossbow +2 (1d8/19-20)

**Tactics:** The GM should use this encounter to teach the players about Ship Roles. Have the players roll initiative for their ship, assign someone to the Helm, someone to the Guns, and someone to Damage Control. The pirates will fire their ballista once, then attempt to ram and board. If two pirates are killed, the remaining pirate surrenders.

---

## Part 2: The Flooded Vault

With the pirates dealt with, the players can tie off their ship and board the precursor vault. The interior is flooded with waist-deep seawater (difficult terrain) and glows with unstable, green aether-light.

### Hazard: The Aether-Coil (Aether-Burn Tutorial)
In the first chamber, a massive, broken precursor engine sparks violently. To progress deeper into the vault and reach the *Gilded Wake's* cargo hold, the players must disable it.
- **Skill Check:** Disable Device or Knowledge (Engineering) DC 15.
- **The Mechanic:** The GM should encourage the player making the check to use the **Aether-Burn** mechanic (Chapter 26). By accepting 1 point of nonlethal Aether-Burn, the player can gain a massive advantage (or reroll a failure).
- **Failure:** If the check fails, the coil discharges, dealing 1d6 electricity damage to everyone in the room (Reflex DC 12 half).

### Looting the Vault
In the adjoining chamber, the players find the corpse of a deep-sea creature and their first piece of Aether-Tech: 
- An **Aether-Pistol** (with 10 charges).
- A **Symbiotic Ship Relic:** *The Drowned Lantern* (taking up the Ring slot). It grants the wearer darkvision, and the ship a 100-foot aura of magical darkness.

---

## Part 3: The Crimson Dominion Arrives (Boss Fight)

As the players reach the cargo hold of the *Gilded Wake* and secure the Consortium's lockbox (which contains a precursor data-drive), they hear the heavy, synchronized stomping of iron boots. 

A Crimson Dominion patrol has boarded the vault from the other side, intent on seizing the precursor technology in the name of the High Admiral.

### Encounter: Dominion Boarding Party
The party consists of two Dominion Legionnaires and their leader, a low-level Dominion Marine.

**Dominion Legionnaires (x2)**
- **CR 1/2** (XP 200 each)
- Human Fighter 1
- **hp** 11 each; **AC** 16 (chain shirt, heavy shield)
- **Melee** Longsword +3 (1d8+2/19-20)

**Sergeant Kael (Dominion Marine)**
- **CR 2** (XP 600)
- Human Fighter (Dominion Marine) 2
- **hp** 22
- **AC** 18 (breastplate, +2 Dex)
- **Melee** Halberd +5 (1d10+4/x3)
- **Special:** *Breach and Clear* (+2 initiative, +2 damage in surprise round/against flat-footed).

**Tactics:** Sergeant Kael orders his men to lock down the players using the cramped, flooded corridors to their advantage. Kael fights to the death, fanatically devoted to the Dominion.

---

## Conclusion & Campaign Hook

Once Kael is defeated, the players can loot his breastplate and halberd, and secure the Consortium data-drive. 

As they return to their ship, the fog lifts, and they see the massive silhouette of a Crimson Dominion dreadnought on the horizon. They must flee into the Unwritten Sea!

**Rewards:**
- The players reach **Level 2**.
- They possess a highly valuable precursor data-drive. They must now decide where to sail:
  - Do they go to **Port Meridian** to sell it back to the Consortium?
  - Do they take it to the **Independent Salvagers** to uncover its secrets?
  - Do they bring it to the **Free Captains** to gain favor with the Pirate King?

The choice is theirs, and the campaign officially begins!
