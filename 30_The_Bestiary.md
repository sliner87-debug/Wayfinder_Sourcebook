# Chapter 30: The Bestiary of the Charted Waters

The Shattered Expanse is home to more than just rival pirate crews. Horrific monstrosities lurk in the abyssal depths, ancient machines guard ruined islands, and the empires of the world deploy terrifying weapons of war. 

This bestiary provides custom stat blocks tailored to the unique factions and environments of the setting.

---

## The Crimson Dominion

### Dominion Inquisitor
*Medium humanoid (human), lawful evil*
**Armor Class:** 15 (studded leather)
**Hit Points:** 71 (11d8 + 22)
**Speed:** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 15 (+2) |

**Saving Throws:** Dex +6, Wis +6
**Skills:** Insight +6, Investigation +5, Perception +6
**Senses:** passive Perception 16
**Languages:** Common, Dominion standard
**Challenge:** 5 (1,800 XP)

**Inquisitor's Monocle:** The inquisitor has advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks, and can cast *zone of truth* (save DC 14) once per day without expending spell slots.
**Aether-Powered Munitions:** The inquisitor's weapon attacks are magical. When the inquisitor hits with any weapon, the weapon deals an extra 1d8 force damage (included in the attack).

#### Actions
**Multiattack:** The inquisitor makes two attacks with their Aether-Rifle or their Rapier.

**Rapier:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) force damage.

**Aether-Rifle:** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) force damage.

**Suppressing Fire (Recharge 5-6):** The inquisitor fires a barrage of aether-bolts in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw, taking 27 (6d8) force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. Any creature that fails the save has its speed halved until the end of its next turn.

#### Reactions
**Parry:** The inquisitor adds 3 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the inquisitor must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.

### Ironclad Juggernaut
*Large humanoid (any race), lawful evil*
**Armor Class:** 19 (Ironclad Plating)
**Hit Points:** 114 (12d10 + 48)
**Speed:** 20 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 20 (+5) | 8 (-1) | 18 (+4) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 9 (-1) |

**Saving Throws:** Str +8, Con +7
**Damage Resistances:** bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
**Condition Immunities:** frightened, poisoned
**Senses:** passive Perception 11
**Languages:** Common
**Challenge:** 8 (3,900 XP)

**Juggernaut Armor:** Any critical hit against the Juggernaut becomes a normal hit. It has disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks and sinks in water.
**Siege Monster:** The Juggernaut deals double damage to objects and structures.

#### Actions
**Multiattack:** The Juggernaut makes two attacks with its Siege Hammer.

**Siege Hammer:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 18 (2d12 + 5) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

**Aether-Vent (Recharge 6):** The Juggernaut vents superheated aether-steam from its armor in a 15-foot radius. Each creature in that area must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw, taking 35 (10d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

## The Drowned Silk Houses

### Drow Deep-Weaver
*Medium humanoid (elf), neutral evil*
**Armor Class:** 15 (mage armor)
**Hit Points:** 54 (12d8)
**Speed:** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 9 (-1) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 17 (+3) | 13 (+1) | 12 (+1) |

**Skills:** Arcana +5, Stealth +6
**Senses:** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 11
**Languages:** Elvish, Undercommon
**Challenge:** 4 (1,100 XP)

**Fey Ancestry:** The drow has advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put the drow to sleep.
**Innate Spellcasting:** The drow's spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 11). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
At will: *dancing lights*
1/day each: *darkness*, *faerie fire*
**Spellcasting:** The drow is a 7th-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 13, +5 to hit with spell attacks). It has the following wizard spells prepared:
Cantrips (at will): *mage hand*, *poison spray*, *ray of frost*
1st level (4 slots): *mage armor*, *magic missile*, *shield*
2nd level (3 slots): *web*, *misty step*
3rd level (3 slots): *vampiric touch*, *counterspell*
4th level (1 slot): *Evard's black tentacles*

#### Actions
**Abyssal Staff:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +1 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 2 (1d6 - 1) bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) necrotic damage.

### Abyssal Thrall
*Medium monstrosity, chaotic evil*
**Armor Class:** 12 (natural armor)
**Hit Points:** 33 (6d8 + 6)
**Speed:** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 13 (+1) | 4 (-3) | 8 (-1) | 5 (-3) |

**Damage Immunities:** poison
**Condition Immunities:** charmed, frightened, poisoned
**Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9
**Languages:** understands Abyssal but can't speak
**Challenge:** 2 (450 XP)

**Reckless:** At the start of its turn, the thrall can gain advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls it makes during that turn, but attack rolls against it have advantage until the start of its next turn.
**Amphibious:** The thrall can breathe air and water.

#### Actions
**Multiattack:** The thrall makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.

**Bite:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage. If the target is a humanoid, it must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or contract the Abyssal Rot disease. Until the disease is cured, the target can't regain hit points except by magical means, and the target's hit point maximum decreases by 3 (1d6) every 24 hours.

**Claws:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.

## Environmental Horrors

### Sentient Kelp Mass
*Huge plant, unaligned*
**Armor Class:** 13 (natural armor)
**Hit Points:** 105 (10d12 + 40)
**Speed:** 10 ft., swim 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 20 (+5) | 8 (-1) | 18 (+4) | 3 (-4) | 10 (+0) | 3 (-4) |

**Damage Vulnerabilities:** fire
**Damage Resistances:** bludgeoning, piercing
**Condition Immunities:** blinded, deafened, frightened, prone
**Senses:** blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), passive Perception 10
**Languages:** -
**Challenge:** 6 (2,300 XP)

**False Appearance:** While the kelp mass remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a normal patch of kelp.
**Water Breathing:** The kelp mass can breathe only underwater.

#### Actions
**Multiattack:** The kelp mass makes two attacks with its grasping vines.

**Grasping Vine:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it is grappled (escape DC 15). The kelp mass has four vines, each of which can grapple one target.

**Reel:** The kelp mass pulls each creature grappled by it up to 10 feet straight toward it.

**Crush:** The kelp mass deals 23 (4d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage to each creature it is grappling.

### Lesser Leviathan Spawn
*Huge dragon, chaotic evil*
**Armor Class:** 17 (natural armor)
**Hit Points:** 161 (14d12 + 70)
**Speed:** 20 ft., swim 60 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 23 (+6) | 14 (+2) | 20 (+5) | 7 (-2) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) |

**Saving Throws:** Con +9, Wis +5
**Damage Resistances:** cold, lightning
**Senses:** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 11
**Languages:** Draconic, Primordial
**Challenge:** 10 (5,900 XP)

**Amphibious:** The spawn can breathe air and water.
**Siege Monster:** The spawn deals double damage to objects and structures.

#### Actions
**Multiattack:** The spawn makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its tail.

**Bite:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 22 (3d10 + 6) piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it is grappled (escape DC 16). Until this grapple ends, the target is restrained, and the spawn can't bite another target.

**Tail:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +10 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit:* 19 (3d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage.

**Swallow:** The spawn makes one bite attack against a Large or smaller creature it is grappling. If the attack hits, the target takes the bite's damage, the target is swallowed, and the grapple ends. While swallowed, the creature is blinded and restrained, it has total cover against attacks and other effects outside the spawn, and it takes 21 (6d6) acid damage at the start of each of the spawn's turns.
If the spawn takes 30 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, the spawn must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate all swallowed creatures.

**Tidal Breath (Recharge 5-6):** The spawn exhales a crushing wave of freezing water in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, taking 45 (10d8) cold damage on a failed save and being pushed 20 feet away and knocked prone. On a successful save, the creature takes half damage and isn't pushed or knocked prone.

## First Captain Relics

### Rogue Aegis Automaton
*Large construct, unaligned*
**Armor Class:** 18 (natural armor)
**Hit Points:** 95 (10d10 + 40)
**Speed:** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 18 (+4) | 10 (+0) | 18 (+4) | 4 (-3) | 10 (+0) | 1 (-5) |

**Damage Immunities:** poison, psychic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks that aren't adamantine
**Condition Immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned
**Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
**Languages:** understands the languages of its creator but can't speak
**Challenge:** 7 (2,900 XP)

**Hard-Light Shielding:** The automaton has a damage threshold of 10. It takes no damage from an attack or effect that deals 10 or less damage.
**Unstable Core:** When the automaton is reduced to 0 hit points, its aether-core violently ruptures. Each creature within 20 feet of it must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

#### Actions
**Multiattack:** The automaton makes two melee attacks.

**Slam:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) force damage.

**Gravity Pulse (Recharge 6):** The automaton stomps the ground, manipulating gravity. Each creature within 30 feet of the automaton must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw. On a failure, the creature takes 22 (4d10) force damage and is thrown 20 feet straight up into the air, falling at the end of the turn and taking falling damage. On a success, the creature takes half damage and isn't thrown.
