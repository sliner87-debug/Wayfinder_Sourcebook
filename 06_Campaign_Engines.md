# Chapter 6: Campaign Engines

The campaign loop is: **Explore → Discover → Record → Understand → Upgrade → Explore Further**. Combat feeds discovery, and discovery changes the world.

## The Discovery System & Captain's Log
A Discovery is a verified, meaningful first-time revelation that expands the world. A rumor is not a discovery; it must be physically mapped, witnessed, or magically confirmed.
- **Wayfinding Lore:** Accumulated understanding. Never spent, only gained. Used to unlock Fusion Techniques, ship awakenings, and legendary status.
- **Discovery Points (DP):** Short-term resource spent on Fusion Techniques and special class abilities (max 3-10, refreshes during downtime).
- **The Captain's Log:** A magical, permanent record. It tracks discoveries across categories (Charts, Species, Trade, Magic, Spirits, Knowledge (History), Ships). The Log contains "Hidden Tags" (e.g., Arcane, Drow, First Captain) tracked by the DM that unlock future transformations or ship upgrades.

### Discovery Tiers
1. **Local:** Small area (e.g., hidden cove). Grants 1 Lore, normal XP.
2. **Regional:** Changes travel/trade for a region (e.g., safe shipping lane). Grants 2 Lore, ship/port benefit.
3. **Major:** Matters to a major faction (e.g., lost civilization outpost). Grants 4 Lore, strong faction reaction, unlock.
4. **World-Shaping:** Changes the map or economy (e.g., Underdark ocean route). Grants 8 Lore, major political shift.
5. **Mythic:** Happens only in legend (e.g., First Captain revelation). Grants 12 Lore, capstone narrative effects.

## The Passage System
The ocean is the dungeon. Passages are the "rooms." A voyage consists of multiple Passages.
1. **Captain's Orders:** Set the priority (Fast, Survey, Hunt, Hidden, Trade, Deep).
2. **The Sea Responds:** DM determines weather, encounters, ruins, or monsters.
3. **The Crew Acts:** Navigators, lookouts, and specialists report.
4. **Player Decisions:** Fight, negotiate, hide, trade, board.
5. **Resolution:** Update supplies, repairs, Lore, and morale.

### Boarding Combat (The Corsair Dungeon)
A hostile ship is a moving dungeon. Boarding has phases: Close distance, Cannon exchange, Hook, Swing across, Deck battle, Officer duel, Capture, Salvage.

## Ship Construction Rules Engine
A ship is built using **Ship Construction Points (SCP)**.
- **Size 1 (Small Craft):** 50 SCP, 1 Hull slot. (Scout/Smuggler).
- **Size 2 (Light Vessel):** 100 SCP, 2 Hull slots. (Piracy/Fast attack).
- **Size 3 (Medium Vessel):** 200 SCP, 3 Hull slots. (Professional pirate ship).
- **Size 4 (Heavy Vessel):** 350 SCP, 5 Hull slots. (Frigate/Galleon).
- **Size 5 (Capital Vessel):** 600 SCP, 8 Hull slots. (Man-o-war).

**Categories & Materials:**
SCP is spent on Structure, Propulsion, Combat, Utility, and Special Systems. Weapons take up slots (e.g., Light Cannon = 1 slot, 5 SCP; Dragonfire Cannon = 5 slots, 75 SCP).
Materials modify Hull Strength, Weight, and Magical Affinity. (e.g., Ironwood adds +40% Hull, +20% Weight; Darkwood adds +15% Hull, -30% Weight).

Ships can install Magical (teleportation anchors), Psionic (thought navigation), Divine (celestial sails), or Spirit (living figureheads) systems.
