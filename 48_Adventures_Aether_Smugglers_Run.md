# Chapter 48: The Aether-Smuggler's Run (Levels 1-3)

*An introductory adventure designed to teach players the basics of Naval Combat, ship management, and the ruthless politics of the Expanse.*

## The Premise
The players begin in the chaotic shantytown of **Shipwreck Cove**. They are flat broke, but they possess a small, dilapidated sloop (Size 1: The Bramble Skiff). A desperate, sweaty Consortium merchant named **Silas Vance** approaches them in the Powder-Keg Tavern with a highly illegal job.

### The Job
Silas has acquired an **Unstable Aether Core** stolen from a Dominion forge. It is highly volatile and highly illegal. He needs it transported immediately to a buyer waiting on a small, uncharted atoll called **Razor-Reef**, a two-day sail away. He promises 500 gp upon delivery.

## Part 1: The Voyage and The Patrol
The players set sail. The DM should use this time to let players test their ship roles (rolling Navigation to plot a course, having the Quartermaster manage rations).

**The Encounter:** On the second day, a **Crimson Dominion Patrol Skiff** (Size 1) spots them. 
- The Dominion ship will fire a warning shot and demand to board and inspect the cargo.
- If the players allow the boarding, a **Dominion Marine** and an **Inquisitor** (See Chapter 35) will search the hold. They will find the core unless the players pass a DC 15 Bluff or Stealth check.
- If combat breaks out, the DM runs a tutorial Naval Combat encounter. The Dominion Skiff has 50 Hull Points and two basic cannons. The goal is to either sink the patrol or damage their sails to escape.

## Part 2: Razor-Reef
The players arrive at the atoll. It is desolate, surrounded by sharp coral. The buyer's ship, a sleek black schooner, is anchored in the lagoon. 

**The Betrayal:** The buyer is not a merchant; it's a **Consortium Repo-Man** (See Chapter 35) named **Kargen**. Silas owed the Consortium money, and Kargen was sent to take the Aether Core and kill the smugglers to tie up loose ends.
- Kargen demands the core and refuses to pay. 
- If the players fight, Kargen signals his crew. 
- **The Fight:** The players must fight Kargen (CR 1) and two **Corsair Thugs** (CR 1/8) on the sandy beach of the atoll. 

## Part 3: The Volatile Core
During the fight (perhaps on Initiative count 0 of the second round), the wooden crate holding the Aether Core is damaged. The core begins to glow bright red and emit a high-pitched whine. 

- The players have 3 rounds to secure the core before it explodes. 
- A player adjacent to the core can use a standard action to make a DC 15 Knowledge (arcana) or Sleight of Hand check to magically stabilize or physically realign the crystal matrix.
- If it explodes, it deals 3d6 force damage to everything in a 20-foot radius (DC 15 Reflex save for half). 

## Conclusion and Rewards
If the players survive and defeat Kargen, they can loot his body (finding a *Smuggler's Pocket* and 100 gp). If they saved the core, they can keep it. An Unstable Aether Core can be sold at a black market (like Shipwreck Cove) for 1,000 gp, or kept to power a future ship upgrade. 

The players have officially made an enemy of the Grand Merchant Consortium, but they have their first real taste of plunder. They should reach Level 2 (or 3, if milestones are used).
