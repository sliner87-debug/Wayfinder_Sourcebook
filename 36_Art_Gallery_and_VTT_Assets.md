# Chapter 36: Art Gallery & VTT Assets

To bring the Shattered Expanse to life at your table or on a Virtual Tabletop (VTT), we have provided this collection of high-quality, atmospheric illustrations representing the core factions, major NPCs, and central mechanics of the setting. 

Each piece of artwork is accompanied by a **VTT Prompt / Read-Aloud Text** that the GM can use to set the scene for the players.

---

## Phase 1: The Faction Hubs

### The Crimson Citadel (The Crimson Dominion)
![The Crimson Citadel](assets/crimson_citadel_1785873825465.jpg)
> **GM Read-Aloud:** *“The fog parts, revealing a staggering display of military might. Carved directly into the jagged, smoking caldera of an active volcano is the Crimson Citadel. The dark-iron fortress looms over a natural harbor that churns with the wakes of heavy dreadnoughts. At the center, the Obsidian Spire pierces the sky, glowing with an eternal, magical red fire that casts a bloody reflection across the water.”*

### Port Meridian (Grand Merchant Consortium)
![Port Meridian](assets/port_meridian_1785873833201.jpg)
> **GM Read-Aloud:** *“Welcome to the wealthiest city in the known world. Port Meridian is a dizzying, sprawling archipelago of towering marble banks, neon-lit arcane bazaars, and countless toll bridges. The docks are packed bow-to-stern with galleons flying the gold-scale flag. The air smells of exotic spices, ozone, and predatory commerce.”*

### The Verdant Expanse (The Spirit Keepers)
![The Verdant Expanse](assets/verdant_expanse_1785873841374.jpg)
> **GM Read-Aloud:** *“The open ocean gives way to a massive, magically accelerated kelp forest that stretches across the surface as far as the eye can see. Sunlight filters through the thick, vibrant green aquatic vines, illuminating ancient druidic structures woven seamlessly into the flora. It is deeply serene, but you can feel the predatory pulse of nature watching your ship.”*

### The Jagged Reefs (Free Captains Alliance)
![The Jagged Reefs](assets/jagged_reefs_1785873849394.jpg)
> **GM Read-Aloud:** *“You navigate the treacherous, shifting sandbars into the heart of the Jagged Reefs. Lightning arcs across the stormy sky, revealing a ramshackle pirate cove hidden within the razor-sharp coral. The buildings here aren't constructed—they are bolted together from the splintered hulls of a hundred wrecked galleons.”*

## Phase 2: The Rogues' Gallery (Major NPCs)

### High Admiral Vane (Crimson Dominion)
![High Admiral Vane](assets/high_admiral_vane_1785873865411.jpg)
> **VTT Character Prompt:** A towering, heavily scarred military commander clad in massive, dark-iron Hellknight plate armor with crimson trim. He wields a glowing, flaming halberd and stares forward with absolute, terrifying authority.

### Pirate King "Hurricane" Jax (Free Captains)
![Hurricane Jax](assets/hurricane_jax_1785873873569.jpg)
> **VTT Character Prompt:** A dynamic, swashbuckling pirate king with a rugged smirk. He is leaping from the rigging of his ship, coat billowing in the wind, dual-wielding a glowing green aether-pistol and a razor-sharp cutlass.

### High Elder Naia (Spirit Keepers)
![High Elder Naia](assets/high_elder_naia_1785873881661.jpg)
> **VTT Character Prompt:** An ancient, Deep-Touched druid with pale translucent skin and large, ink-black eyes. She is encased in jagged, living coral armor, and swirling, glowing water orbits her hands.

### Matron Viconia (Drowned Silk Houses)
![Matron Viconia](assets/matron_viconia_1785873889756.jpg)
> **VTT Character Prompt:** A terrifying drow matriarch with white hair and elegant abyssal silk robes. She sits astride a massive, undead abyssal spider, wielding a glowing green venomous whip in the dark trenches.

### The Vance Brothers (Consortium & Salvagers)
![The Vance Brothers](assets/the_vance_brothers_1785873906728.jpg)
> **VTT Character Prompt:** A dual portrait of fierce rivals. On the left, High Factor Vance, wealthy and immaculate in opulent silk, holding a pristine aether-pistol. On the right, his estranged brother, Chief Archaeologist Silas Vance, a scrappy half-elf in leather armor laden with unstable, glowing precursor grenades.
