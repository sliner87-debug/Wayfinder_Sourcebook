# Chapter 41: Bastion (The Crimson Capital)

*The impenetrable heart of the Crimson Dominion, built atop the jagged, smoking caldera of an active volcano.*

Bastion is not a city of leisure; it is a fortress of industry and war. Black smoke constantly chokes the sky above the island, venting from the massive Aether-forges that power the Dominion's ironclad fleet. The city is tiered, with the lowest levels reserved for laborers and the slave-pits, and the upper echelons housing the High Admiralty and the Inquisition.

### Physical Description
The island itself is sheer, black volcanic rock. There are no natural beaches; ships must enter through heavily fortified sea-gates into an artificial, cavernous harbor carved directly into the caldera. The architecture is brutalist—heavy iron, dark stone, and jagged spikes designed to repel boarding actions even on land.

### Key NPCs
- **High Admiral Vane:** The supreme commander of the Dominion forces. He rarely leaves the High Admiralty Spire unless personally leading a fleet. (See Chapter 37 for statblock).
- **Grand Inquisitor Thorne:** A fanatic who oversees the Aether-Processing facilities and ensures that no unsanctioned magic users exist within Bastion's walls.

### Local Laws & Dangers
- **Absolute Prohibition on Unsanctioned Magic:** Casting a spell in Bastion without an official Dominion writ is a capital offense. Inquisitor patrols use *Inquisitor's Lenses* to constantly scan the crowds.
- **The Ash-Sickness:** The air in the lower tiers is highly toxic. Creatures without a filter-mask or immunity to poison who spend more than 24 hours in the lower city must succeed on a DC 15 Fortitude save or become fatigued.

### Points of Interest

#### 1. The Aether-Forges
Located deep within the volcano's caldera, these massive foundries harness geothermal heat and raw Aether to smelt Dominion steel. It is deafeningly loud and incredibly dangerous. Slaves and political prisoners are worked to death here. *Adventure Hook: The players must infiltrate the forges to sabotage a prototype Dreadnought engine before it can be installed.*

#### 2. The High Admiralty Spire
The tallest structure in Bastion, a jagged needle of black iron piercing the smog. The upper floors contain the strategic war rooms where the Dominion plans its conquest of the Expanse. It is heavily guarded by Aegis Automatons and elite Marines.

#### 3. The Drowned Oubliette
The Dominion's prison is built into the sea-wall. At high tide, the lower cells completely flood. High-value prisoners are kept in suspended cages just above the water line, exposed to the freezing spray and the psychological torture of the rising tides.
