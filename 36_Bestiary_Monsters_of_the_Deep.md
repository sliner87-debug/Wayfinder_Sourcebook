# Chapter 36: Bestiary - Monsters of the Deep

This chapter provides 5e-compatible statblocks for the wild, unaligned horrors of the Shattered Expanse. These creatures represent the environmental dangers of a world warped by unstable magic and deep-sea gigantism.

---

### Drowned Sailor
*Medium undead, unaligned*
- **Armor Class:** 10, touch 10, flat-footed 10
- **Hit Points:** 15 (2d8 + 6)
- **Speed:** 20 ft., swim 30 ft.
- **Challenge:** 1/4 (50 XP)

**STR:** 14 (+2) | **DEX:** 10 (+0) | **CON:** 16 (+3) | **INT:** 3 (-4) | **WIS:** 8 (-1) | **CHA:** 5 (-3)
- **Base Atk:** +1 | **Grapple:** +3
> **[PF1E Conversion Sidebar]**
> **CMB:** +3 | **CMD:** 13
- **Feats:** Toughness, Power Attack
- **Damage Immunities:** poison
- **Condition Immunities:** poisoned

**Traits:**
- *Waterlogged:* The Drowned Sailor has resistance to fire damage but disadvantage on dexterity saving throws.
- *Death Throes (Bilge Vomit):* When the Drowned Sailor drops to 0 hit points, it ruptures, violently expelling putrid, black bilge water. Each creature within 5 feet must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of its next turn.

**Actions:**
- *Bloated Slam:* Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage plus 2 (1d4) necrotic damage.

- **Base Atk:** +22 | **Grapple:** +36
> **[PF1E Conversion Sidebar]**
> **CMB:** +36 | **CMD:** 46
- **Feats:** Toughness, Power Attack
- **Saving Throws:** Str +18, Con +18, Wis +12
- **Skills:** Spot +12, Listen +12, Search +12
> **[PF1E Conversion Sidebar]**
> **PF1E Skills:** Perception +12
- **Damage Immunities:** cold, fire, lightning; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Condition Immunities:** charmed, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, stunned
- **Senses:** truesight 120 ft., passive Perception 22

**Traits:**
- *Legendary Resistance (3/Day):* If the Leviathan fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
- *Siege Monster:* The Leviathan deals triple damage to objects, structures, and ships.
- *Water Breathing:* The Leviathan can only breathe underwater, but can hold its breath for 4 hours.

- *Multiattack:* The Leviathan makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its tentacles.

- *Tidal Wave (Recharge 6):* The Leviathan creates a massive tsunami. Each creature and ship within 300 feet of the Leviathan must make a DC 24 Strength saving throw, taking 77 (14d10) bludgeoning damage on a failed save and being pushed 100 feet away and knocked prone.

**Legendary Actions (3/Turn):**
- *Move:* The Leviathan moves up to half its swim speed.
- *Tentacle Attack:* The Leviathan makes one tentacle attack.
- *Deep Sea Roar (Costs 2 Actions):* The Leviathan emits a terrifying roar. Each creature within 120 feet of the Leviathan and able to hear it must succeed on a DC 20 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened for 1 minute.
