# Chapter 21: Regional Gazetteer - The Drowned Silk Houses

*“The surface-dwellers fight over scraps of driftwood and claim they rule the ocean. They do not realize the true power lies in the crushing dark, where their wooden toys splinter and their kings drown.”* — Matron Viconia of House Xorlarrin

The Drowned Silk Houses are not a surface nation; they are an ancient, predatory civilization operating primarily within the Sunless Sea (the Underdark Ocean). They are a fractured society of Drow noble houses who survived a cataclysmic flood centuries ago by adapting their magic to the deep ocean. They view the surface world as a hunting ground, a political chessboard, and a resource pool to be exploited from the shadows.

## 1. Geography, Demographics & Hubs

The Silk Houses exist in a perpetual state of twilight, occupying massive caverns and bioluminescent trenches beneath the ocean floor. 

**Population Overview:**
- Total Estimated Population: ~800,000 (Drow and deep-sea races)
- The Matron Mothers & Nobles: 2%
- The Crimson Guard & Arcane Scholars: 28%
- The Thralls (Enslaved surface races & lesser aquatic beings): 70%

### Nymorath Deep Harbor (Capital Hub)
Nymorath is a breathtaking and terrifying city built into the walls of a massive underwater trench, illuminated by bioluminescent fungi and chained deep-sea leviathans. A massive sphere of magically maintained air encompasses the noble districts, while the lower slums and slave pens are partially flooded, forcing thralls to rely on water-breathing enchantments to survive. 
- **The Obsidian Web:** The central political arena where the Matron Mothers hold court, negotiate alliances, and plot assassinations.
- **The Abyssal Forges:** Where Drow artificers craft weapons from black coral and deep-sea metals that shatter surface steel.
- **The Drown-Gates:** Massive, magical whirlpool-portals that allow their fleets to instantly surface in the Charted Waters for raids.

### The Trench Outposts
Scattered listening posts and forward operating bases located near major surface trade routes, used to monitor the Merchant Consortium and Crimson Dominion.

## 2. Magic Systems Integration

The Drow possess the most advanced, ancient, and dangerous understanding of magic in the Shattered Expanse.

- **Deep Magic (The Foundation):** The Drow are the undisputed masters of Deep Magic. They use it to manipulate gravity, control water pressure, and mutate deep-sea beasts into war mounts. It is inherently tied to darkness and cold.
- **Arcane Magic (Weaponized):** Highly developed, but focused on stealth, illusion, and assassination. Necromancy is widely practiced, utilizing drowned sailors as cheap, mindless labor.
- **Psionics (Espionage):** Used extensively by their elite spies to mind-read captured captains and communicate silently during surface raids. 
- **Spirit Magic (Corrupted):** They capture ocean spirits and systematically torture and corrupt them, twisting them into dark entities to power their submarine fleets.

## 3. Faction Relations (Geopolitical Matrix)

The Drow do not seek open war; they seek absolute, unseen control.

- **Grand Merchant Consortium:** *Lucrative Exploitation.* The Drow officially do not exist according to the Consortium, but in reality, the two factions run massive, secret black-market smuggling rings. The Drow sell ancient artifacts and poisons for surface luxuries and slaves.
- **Crimson Dominion:** *The Shadow War.* The Dominion is the only surface power that poses a military threat. The Drow actively sabotage Dominion shipyards, sink their fleets in "unexplained storms," and assassinate their mages to keep them fractured.
- **Free Captains Alliance:** *Convenient Pawns.* The Drow frequently manipulate pirate fleets into attacking Dominion or Consortium targets by leaking false maps or rumors, using them as unwitting mercenaries.
- **Spirit Keepers:** *Hated Enemies.* The Keepers actively try to heal the ocean spirits the Drow corrupt. The Drow view the Keepers as naive fools and will slaughter them on sight.
- **Independent Salvagers:** *Prey.* The Drow consider all First Captain artifacts in the Unwritten Sea to be their rightful property. They actively hunt Salvager vessels that delve too deep.

## 4. Societal Hierarchy (The Matriarchy)

Drow society is a brutal, paranoid matriarchy where advancement is achieved through betrayal and assassination.

1. **The Matron Mothers:** The absolute rulers of the Silk Houses. High Priestesses of Deep Magic who command unquestioned obedience.
2. **The Noble Houses:** The extended families of the Matrons. They constantly war with one another in secret for political favor.
3. **The Crimson Guard:** Elite, fanatically loyal male warriors and assassins who serve the Matrons.
4. **The Male Arcane Scholars:** While denied true political power, male wizards are highly valued for their magical research and tactical knowledge.
5. **The Thralls:** The enslaved underclass. Comprised of captured surface sailors, tritons, and Kuo-toa. They are considered disposable and are often used as biological experiments.

## 5. Internal Politics & Rival Factions

The Silk Houses are united only in their hatred of the surface; internally, they are a snake pit.

- **House Xorlarrin vs. House Baenre:** A centuries-old blood feud between the two most powerful houses. Xorlarrin favors aggressive surface raids, while Baenre prefers economic manipulation and smuggling.
- **The Bregan D'aerthe (The Mercenaries):** A rogue faction of exiled male nobles and elite assassins who operate independently, selling their services to the highest bidder (even surface dwellers).
- **The Cult of the First Captains:** A heretical, underground religious movement that believes the First Captains were actually ancient Drow gods, and seeks to awaken the slumbering horrors of the Unwritten Sea to wipe out the surface completely.

## 6. The Military Machine

The Drow do not fight fair. They strike from the shadows, cripple their enemies, and vanish before a retaliation can be organized.

- **Naval Fleet Composition:** They do not use wooden sailing ships. They utilize biological submarines—massive, hollowed-out, undead sea leviathans (like giant armored squids or skeletal whales) piloted by Necromancers. 
- **Combat Tactics:** They attack from below. Their "ships" breach the surface, deploying elite boarding parties that use innate darkness magic to blind the enemy crew. 
- **Shipboard Weaponry:** They do not use cannons. They use sonic projectors that can shatter wooden hulls, and magical torpedoes that rapidly freeze the water around an enemy ship, trapping it in ice.
