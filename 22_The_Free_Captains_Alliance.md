# Chapter 22: Regional Gazetteer - The Free Captains Alliance

*“The Dominion claims the ocean by divine right. The Consortium claims it by contract. We claim it because our sails are full, our guns are loaded, and the horizon belongs to whoever is bold enough to chase it.”* — Pirate King "Hurricane" Jax

The Free Captains Alliance is not a government; it is a chaotic, rowdy, and fiercely independent confederacy of pirate lords, privateers, explorers, and exiles. They are bound together not by laws, but by a shared hatred of empire and a mutual agreement known as "The Corsair's Code." To the rest of the world, they are lawless terrorists. To themselves, they are the only truly free souls on the Shattered Expanse.

## 1. Geography, Demographics & Hubs

The Alliance operates out of the **Jagged Reefs**, a vast, treacherous maze of razor-sharp coral and shifting sandbars that only the most skilled (or magically guided) navigators can traverse without tearing their hulls open.

**Population Overview:**
- Total Estimated Population: ~400,000 (Highly transient)
- The Pirate Lords (The Brethren Court): <1%
- Wayfinders, Privateers, & Sworn Crews: 60%
- Smugglers, Tavern-Keepers, & Fence Merchants: 39%

### Blackwake Harbor (Capital Hub)
Blackwake Harbor is a massive, floating city that shouldn't exist. It is constructed entirely from lashed-together galleons, ancient driftwood, and salvaged hulls suspended within a calm lagoon in the center of the Jagged Reefs. It is a city of rope bridges, swinging lanterns, and perpetual celebration (or brawling).
- **The Brethren's Table:** A massive, hollowed-out hull of a first-rate ship-of-the-line where the Pirate Lords meet to vote on the rare occasions the Alliance needs to act as a unified fleet.
- **The Powder Keg:** The chaotic tavern district, where fortunes are won and lost on games of dice, and where most crews are recruited.
- **The Chop Shop:** A massive drydock dedicated to refitting stolen ships, scrubbing their identifying marks, and outfitting them with illegal weaponry.

### The Hidden Coves
The Alliance maintains dozens of secret inlets, hidden coves, and disguised anchorages throughout the Charted Waters, allowing them to strike and vanish before the Dominion or Consortium can respond.

## 2. Magic Systems Integration

The Free Captains are highly pragmatic about magic; if it keeps the ship afloat or helps them steal gold, they use it.

- **Spirit Magic (The Lifeline):** The most common and respected magic among the Captains. Many crews employ shamans to speak with the winds, beg safe passage from minor ocean spirits, and bond with Navigator Spirits to guide them through the Reefs.
- **Arcane Magic (Stolen & Scavenged):** They rarely have formally trained wizards. Instead, they rely on rogue sorcerers, stolen wands, and scavenged scrolls. Their magic is chaotic, loud, and unpredictable.
- **Deep Magic (Taboo):** Most Captains are highly superstitious and consider the deep ocean cursed. Those who practice Deep Magic are viewed with intense suspicion and are often accused of making deals with the Drow.
- **Psionics (Untrusted):** Psions are rarely found on pirate ships. The concept of someone reading thoughts violates the core tenet of piratical freedom; mind-readers are usually thrown overboard.

## 3. Faction Relations (Geopolitical Matrix)

The Alliance views the world through a lens of targets, threats, and temporary allies.

- **Crimson Dominion:** *Kill on Sight.* The Dominion represents everything the Captains hate: slavery, conformity, and imperial control. The Alliance will actively target Dominion slaver ships and border forts.
- **Grand Merchant Consortium:** *Primary Targets (But Occasional Employers).* Consortium treasure galleons are the ultimate prize. However, the Captains are pragmatic; they will gladly accept a Consortium "Letter of Marque" to act as privateers against the Dominion, provided the gold is good.
- **Drowned Silk Houses (Drow):** *Superstitious Terror.* The Free Captains rarely engage the Drow willingly. When ships go missing in calm waters, the pirates blame the Drow. 
- **Spirit Keepers:** *Wary Respect.* The Keepers are one of the few factions the Pirates try not to anger. A pirate captain knows better than to hunt a leviathan in Keeper territory, lest the ocean itself turn against them.
- **Independent Salvagers:** *Friendly Rivals.* The Captains frequently trade with the Salvagers. They often race each other into the Unwritten Sea to claim First Captain wrecks.

## 4. Societal Hierarchy (The Meritocracy)

In Blackwake Harbor, nobility means nothing. You are only worth the strength of your sword arm, the loyalty of your crew, and the size of your ship.

1. **The Pirate Lords:** The most feared and successful captains in the Expanse. They command massive fleets (armadas) and hold a seat on the Brethren Court.
2. **The Wayfinders / Captains:** Independent ship commanders. Within the Alliance, every Captain is considered a sovereign ruler of their own deck.
3. **The Quartermasters:** Highly respected officers elected by the crew to represent their interests, distribute loot, and ensure the Captain doesn't abuse their power.
4. **The Sworn Crew:** The sailors who have sworn the Corsair's Code. They are guaranteed a share of the plunder and the right to vote on the ship's destination.
5. **The Scallywags (Landlubbers):** The un-sworn. Merchants, tavern keeps, and dockworkers who live in Blackwake Harbor but do not sail.

## 5. Internal Politics & Rival Factions

The Alliance is constantly on the verge of tearing itself apart, held together only by external threats.

- **The Old Guard:** Traditional pirate lords who believe the Alliance should focus purely on raiding the Consortium and enjoying their freedom.
- **The Abolitionists:** Radical captains who believe the Alliance's primary goal should be a full-scale war to destroy the Crimson Dominion and free the slave caste.
- **The Traitor Captains:** Crews who secretly accept pardons and paychecks from the Dominion to hunt down other pirates (the ultimate sin in Blackwake).
- **The Mutiny Problem:** Every Captain is one bad voyage away from being overthrown by their own crew. Democracy on a pirate ship is a double-edged sword.

## 6. The Military Machine

The Free Captains do not have a uniform standing navy. They fight a guerrilla war at sea.

- **Naval Fleet Composition:** A chaotic mix of everything. Stolen Dominion frigates, retrofitted Consortium merchantmen, and custom-built, impossibly fast blockade runners. No two ships look alike.
- **Combat Tactics:** Speed and surprise. They rely on ambush tactics, utilizing thick fog (often magically summoned), narrow straits, and boarding actions. They prefer to capture ships intact rather than sink them.
- **Shipboard Weaponry:** A mix of traditional black-powder cannons (which they favor for their reliability), scavenged Arcane Ballistae, and whatever experimental, highly volatile First Captain weaponry they managed to salvage.
