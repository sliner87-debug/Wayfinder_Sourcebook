# Chapter 34: Rogues' Gallery

The following NPCs are the major power players of the Shattered Expanse. They can serve as endgame adversaries, uneasy allies, or powerful patrons. Their statistics are built using the Pathfinder 1st Edition ruleset, designed to challenge high-level parties.

---

## High Admiral Vane (The Crimson Dominion)
Vane is a towering, heavily scarred human who commands the Crimson Dominion's fleets with absolute, terrifying authority. He wields a massive, aether-charged halberd and is clad in impenetrable hellknight plate.

**High Admiral Vane** (CR 17)
XP 102,400
LE Medium humanoid (human)
Fighter (Dominion Marine) 10 / Hellknight 8
**Init** +6; **Senses** Perception +18
**Aura** fear (10 ft., DC 19)
**DEFENSE**
**AC** 33, touch 13, flat-footed 31 (+14 armor, +2 Dex, +2 deflection, +5 natural)
**hp** 211 (18 HD; 10d10+8d10+108)
**Fort** +19, **Ref** +9, **Will** +11 (+15 vs charm/compulsion)
**Defensive Abilities** Ironclad Advance, Force of Will; **Immune** difficult terrain, fire (120 points/day)
**OFFENSE**
**Speed** 30 ft. (20 ft. in armor)
**Melee** *+3 axiomatic flaming burst heavy halberd* +31/+26/+21/+16 (1d10+16/19-20 x3 plus 1d6 fire and 2d6 vs chaotic)
**Special Attacks** Breach and Clear, Relentless Assault, Smite Chaos 3/day (+4 attack, +8 damage), Hellknight Disciplines (Fearsome, Wrack)
**TACTICS**
**During Combat** Vane uses his halberd to keep enemies at reach. He uses *Smite Chaos* on the most obvious threat (usually a Free Captain or rogue caster) and uses *Fearsome* to demoralize opponents when he strikes them, leveraging his massive Intimidate bonus.
**STATISTICS**
**Str** 24, **Dex** 14, **Con** 20, **Int** 13, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 14
**Base Atk** +18; **CMB** +25; **CMD** 39
**Feats** Combat Reflexes, Critical Focus, Dazzling Display, Improved Critical (halberd), Improved Initiative, Iron Will, Lunge, Power Attack, Shatter Defenses, Stand Still, Toughness, Weapon Focus (halberd), Weapon Specialization (halberd)
**Skills** Intimidate +30, Knowledge (engineering) +18, Perception +18, Sense Motive +18
**Languages** Common, Infernal
**Gear** *+5 hellknight plate*, *+3 axiomatic flaming burst halberd*, *belt of physical might +4 (Str, Con)*, *ring of protection +2*, *amulet of natural armor +3*.

## High Factor Vance of the Gold Scale (Consortium)
Vance is a shrewd, immaculately dressed merchant lord. He is the estranged older brother of Silas Vance (leader of the Salvagers). While Silas digs for history, High Factor Vance sells it. He rarely fights directly, preferring to bribe, manipulate, or use expensive aether-tech to disable foes.

**High Factor Vance** (CR 14)
XP 38,400
LN Medium humanoid (human)
Rogue (Smuggler-Baron) 15
**Init** +5; **Senses** Perception +20
**DEFENSE**
**AC** 26, touch 18, flat-footed 21 (+6 armor, +5 Dex, +3 deflection, +2 insight)
**hp** 123 (15d8+45)
**Fort** +8, **Ref** +15, **Will** +9
**Defensive Abilities** Evasion, Improved Uncanny Dodge, Trap Sense +5
**OFFENSE**
**Speed** 30 ft.
**Melee** *+2 keen rapier* +18/+13/+8 (1d6+2/15-20)
**Ranged** *+2 distance aether-pistol* +18/+13/+8 (1d6+2/x4)
**Special Attacks** Sneak Attack +8d6, Combat Bribery (DC 22), Market Manipulation
**TACTICS**
**During Combat** Vance uses *Combat Bribery* to fascinate the heaviest hitters while he positions himself. He relies on his *Aether-Tech Arsenal* to pull specific situational magic items (like a *gem of brightness* or *bead of force*) from his coat. If cornered, he uses *Market Manipulation* to guarantee a critical hit with his aether-pistol or force an enemy to critically miss.
**STATISTICS**
**Str** 10, **Dex** 20, **Con** 14, **Int** 18, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 16
**Base Atk** +11; **CMB** +11; **CMD** 29
**Feats** Agile Maneuvers, Combat Expertise, Deceitful, Improved Feint, Iron Will, Point-Blank Shot, Precise Shot, Weapon Finesse
**Skills** Appraise +25, Bluff +28, Diplomacy +25, Disable Device +23, Knowledge (local) +22, Perception +20, Sense Motive +20, Sleight of Hand +25
**Languages** Common, Aquan, Aether-Code, Elven, Dwarven
**Gear** *+4 shadow leather armor*, *+2 keen rapier*, *+2 distance aether-pistol*, *cloak of resistance +3*, *ring of protection +3*, *headband of vast intelligence +4*, 25,000 gp worth of assorted gems and aether-tech trinkets in his coat.

Matron Viconia rules the deep trenches with terrifying grace. She rides a massive, undead abyssal spider and wields the crushing pressure of the deep as easily as a blade.

**Immune** sleep, pressure; **Resist** cold 10; **SR** 27

## Pirate King "Hurricane" Jax (Free Captains Alliance)
Jax is a whirlwind of steel, gunpowder, and roaring laughter. He fights dual-wielding an aether-pistol and a razor-sharp cutlass, leaping across rigging and swinging on ropes with impossible agility.

**"Hurricane" Jax** (CR 15)
XP 51,200
CN Medium humanoid (human)
Swashbuckler 10 / Gunslinger (Aether-Corsair) 6
**Init** +9; **Senses** Perception +16
**DEFENSE**
**AC** 28, touch 19, flat-footed 20 (+6 armor, +7 Dex, +2 dodge, +3 deflection)
**hp** 142 (16 HD; 10d10+6d10+48)
**Fort** +10, **Ref** +19, **Will** +6
**Defensive Abilities** Charmed Life 5/day, Nimble +2
**OFFENSE**
**Speed** 30 ft.
**Melee** *+3 speed cutlass* +25/+25/+20/+15 (1d6+10/18-20)
**Ranged** *+2 reliable aether-pistol* +24/+19/+14 (1d6+2/x4)
**Special Attacks** Swashbuckler Weapon Training +2, Corsair's Deeds (Boarding Leap, Hull-Breaker), Panache (5/day), Grit (5/day)
**TACTICS**
**During Combat** Jax is never still. He uses *Boarding Leap* to fly around the battlefield, ignoring difficult terrain. He dual-wields, utilizing Two-Weapon Fighting to fire his pistol at touch AC and slash with his speed cutlass in a dizzying barrage. If attacked in melee, he uses *Opportune Parry and Riposte* to deflect blows and strike back.
**STATISTICS**
**Str** 12, **Dex** 24, **Con** 14, **Int** 10, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 16
**Base Atk** +16; **CMB** +17; **CMD** 36
**Feats** Combat Reflexes, Deadly Aim, Point-Blank Shot, Precise Shot, Rapid Reload, Slashing Grace (cutlass), Two-Weapon Fighting, Weapon Finesse, Weapon Focus (cutlass)
**Skills** Acrobatics +26, Climb +20, Profession (sailor) +20, Swim +20
**Gear** *+4 leather armor*, *+3 speed cutlass*, *+2 reliable aether-pistol*, *belt of incredible dexterity +6*.
